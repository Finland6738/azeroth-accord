### Added

- M10.9: **PvP and world rallies** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). A new **Rallies** page in the **Events** view: any member calls a rally (PvP or world) with a meeting point in words, a start within 2 hours and a duration of 15 minutes to 2 hours, and the others answer **Coming** or **Not coming**, visible to everyone. One open rally per character, at most 4 an hour, held by the caller's client and again by every receiver; only the caller changes or closes it. It expires by itself on every client and is removed 10 minutes later. No position is read and no coordinate or map is sent. A chat notice for a new rally and a line in the Action Center; the own rallies and answers in *What I share*. The data set `rally` is short-lived and first hand, like the group finder's; who logs in later asks with the new message `RASK`, and the answers only when the page is opened. Traffic in the simulator (50 clients, 9 rallies with 146 answers in 45 minutes): about 25 B/min per client more on average (SPEC-14 §11).
- M10.8: the **availability map** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). The new **Availability** view (`/accord availability`) shows when the guild usually plays, by day and hour of the realm's week: each client counts **only the minutes its account is online**, over the last 8 weeks, reduced to 4 levels per hour; nothing about raids or attendance is recorded or sent. Everybody sees how many members are usually online in each hour, **who** they are only officers and the Guild Master, and everybody their own; with FDCP as the source, officers also read the days declared in FDCP, labelled as a declaration. **Share when I usually play** (on by default, in Settings and *What I share*) withdraws the shared map when switched off, while the local count goes on. The new data set `avail` is published at most once a week (once a day in the first week). Traffic, in the simulator: inside the budget of 450 B/min in a guild in step, **over it in the first exchange** (530 B/min on average), a peak the owner accepted on 2026-11-26 (SPEC-10 Q26, R17: up to about 550 B/min while the maps are first exchanged).
- M10.7: **bench and composition** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). In the **Events** view officers and the Guild Master place who signed up (the game calendar's yes and tentative, and the matched Raid-Helper sign-ups; a Declined given in the game wins) **To raid** or **To bench**, one place per player, against the target composition; when a role is short the players with that role as **MS2** are offered and can be placed as such. Every member sees their own place, the raid and the bench. **Write to calendar**, after a confirming second click, writes the raid as confirmed and the bench as standby for the members who have not answered in the game, never over an answer they gave (SPEC-10 Q25; an answer given with another character of the account now counts too, for the Raid-Helper write as well), at most 40 per confirmation. The Discord and CSV export of an event has a line-up column. With FDCP as the source Accord writes, accepts and shows no line-up of its own and the view shows FDCP's open night where it gives one. New record `C:` of `raidev` (officers only, 2 days after the event it goes); `Data/Composition.lua`; English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_composition.lua`; SPEC-14 §5.5, §6.6, §7, §13; SPEC-03 §11; the wiki pages *Events*, *Raid-Helper events* and *Privacy and Data* in both languages. **Not checked in the real game.**
- M10.6: **Raid-Helper events from Discord** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). In the **Events** view officers and the Guild Master press **Import from Raid-Helper** and paste the export of an event, the JSON of its API v4 or its CSV: the reader takes the title, the date and time (turned into the realm's time) and every sign-up (Discord name, class and spec, role; signed up, late, bench, tentative or absence), ignores what it does not know, refuses broken, unknown or oversized text with a clear message and never runs anything from it. Sign-ups are matched to members by a hand match made before, the Discord name of the contacts (the game's and the shared one) or a character name; all the characters of a player count once and the sign-up goes to the one of its class. Names that match nobody are matched by hand and remembered; they and the hand matches stay on the officer's computer only, and the unmatched names are removed 2 days after the event (SPEC-10 Q22). The imported event reaches the guild (record `H:` of `raidev`: matched members only, from officers only, 2 days after the event it goes): every member sees their own Discord state; officers also the sign-ups, the roles next to the season roster and the target composition, who is on Discord but not in the game calendar, the reverse and who answered differently. **Put into the game calendar**, after a confirming second click, uses the guild event at that date and time or creates a guild sign-up event, invites the matched members and writes their state where the client lets the officer, never over an answer given in the game (Q25), one call at a time, at most 40 members per confirmation, stopping at the first error and saying who was left out; a member whose answer is missing finds a line in the Action Center. **Export** gives the event's sign-ups back as Discord text or CSV. Accord never contacts Discord or Raid-Helper. Raid-Helper's current export and the calendar calls that write are **not verified** (no real export, no real client). New `Data/RaidHelperText.lua`, `Data/RaidHelper.lua`, `UI/RaidHelperBox.lua`; the writing calls in `Compat.Calendar` and in the fake game calendar of the tests; the size limit of `raidev` is 12 KB for `H:` records (512 bytes for the others); English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_raid_helper.lua`; SPEC-14 §5.5, §6, §11, §13; SPEC-03 §11; the wiki page *Raid-Helper events* in both languages.
- M10.5: **events and sign-ups on the game calendar** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). The new **Events** view (`/accord events`) lists the guild events of the game calendar from 7 days ago to 14 days ahead, whoever created them, with your answer. Choosing one reads everybody's answers from the calendar (signed up, tentative, declined, with the role each one chose in Accord). **Sign up**, **Tentative** and **Decline** write your answer into the game calendar itself, so the game, Finland Dragon Credit Points and the other addons see the same answer; an answer given in the game's calendar shows in Accord. Accord opens one event at a time and never while the game's calendar window is open, an action is pending, in combat or during a boss; if the game refuses the answer from an addon, its calendar window opens instead. The role (tank, healer, melee, ranged), which the game calendar cannot hold, is kept by Accord and preselected from your season MS1, from MS2 when MS1's role is already covered for the target composition and MS2's is not, or from your class, so a member without a season declaration signs up all the same. Only when the client has no usable calendar, officers and the Guild Master create events in Accord, always marked **Not in the game calendar**, and members answer there. During a boss, Accord sends only its short urgent messages (SPEC-14 §10). The values of the calendar's answer codes are not in the Forever API dump: they are in one constant, to check in game. New data set `raidev` (roles, events of the addon and answers to them; 2 days after the event they go); the own roles and answers appear in *What I share*. English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_calendar.lua` with a fake game calendar; SPEC-14 §5, §10; SPEC-03 §11; the wiki page *Events* in both languages.
- M10.4: the **season roster for officers** (behind the switch `season`, on since M10.10). In the **Season** view everybody sees, for the current season of the chosen source, the raiders per role by MS1 (tank, healer, melee, ranged), what MS2 would add, the gaps against a **target composition** and how many MS2 would cover; "Not sure yet" answers apart; the accounts **without a declaration** in a list of their own, never as a gap. Officers and the Guild Master set the target (raid size 10, 20, 25 or 40 and the four roles; the record `target` of `guildcfg`) and **export** the shown declarations as CSV or Discord text in the officers' export box. With FDCP as the source the counts and the export come from its pool: inactive mains are not counted and a member's client, which gets no MS2, shows no MS2 cover. `Data/Composition.lua`; English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_season_view.lua`; SPEC-14 §4.5, §4.6, §7.1; SPEC-03 §8; the wiki page *Season roster* in both languages.
- M10.3: the **season roster** (behind the new switch `season`, on since M10.10). Officers and the Guild Master (`Raid.WRITERS`, SPEC-10 Q21) open a season with a name, which closes the previous one, or close the current one. The view also holds the choice of the season roster's source and the section *Raid and loot addons in the guild*. `Data/Specs.lua` lists each base class's trees and allowed roles (names to check in game in M10.10); the own declarations appear in *What I share*. ## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-25

The first release, **0.8.0**. It covers the milestones **M1 to M8**: foundations, roster, professions and recipes, reputation, guild bank, settings and Italian interface with the look of Finland Dragon Credit Points, data trust and officer tools.

- **The in-game checks were not run before this release**, by the owner's decision (2026-11-25): nothing here has been verified on WoW Forever, only offline. The checklists in `docs/checklists/` stay open, and what they find is fixed in `v0.8.<F>` releases.

- M8.2: the observed timeline ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §2). The records travel only on `OFFICER` or by whisper to an officer (`officerOnly`, checked again when the message leaves the queue, never saved in the outbox, never accepted from `GUILD`), and only once the client has seen an addon message arrive on `OFFICER` in the session: before that they stay on the computer, no whisper fallback; their summary is the new `m` field of `OHELLO`, by month (`Sync:Groups("officer")`), compared by the designated officer, and a record goes out at most every 10 minutes. A demoted officer (no officer character of the account in the guild) loses the officer data and the snapshot. - M7.7: hardening against bad peers and very large guilds ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §6). The codec drops a decoded table deeper than 8 levels (`depth`) and reports how many bytes it decoded; a `REC` with more than 100 records is dropped whole; per sender, one `HELLO`/`BHELLO` a minute and 10 requests of every set a minute are handled, and a sender that makes the client decode more than 256 KB in a minute or sends more than 50 refused records in 10 minutes is in quarantine for 10 minutes (its messages are dropped before decoding; `/accord debug` counts the senders in quarantine, never names them; the counters start again at a change of guild). Above 200 authors a client sends the block summary `BHELLO` (96 blocks of 3 bytes, about 440 B; an author's block is a Fibonacci hash of its key, because the high bits of `Hash.Fast` are nearly the same for the characters of one realm: SPEC-11 §6.4 said 128 blocks from the high bits, changed after the simulator measured both) instead of the per-author `HELLO`, once every officer heard runs a version that knows it; only the designated officer (and a character of the sender's own account) answers with a `DIGEST` by whisper, at most one every 2 s; the first `HELLO` after login waits 10 + random(0, 20 + authors / 10) seconds. The per-author summary is now kept record by record (only the authors of a changed record are hashed again; it is read whole before each `HELLO` sent and after an import, the demo or cleared data), and a `BHELLO` in step with the client is recognised by comparing one string. An officer counts for the choice between `HELLO` and `BHELLO` only once its own `HELLO` has told its version (heard first through a `REC`, as after a server restart, it made nearly every member fall back to the 11 KB per-author `HELLO`). An early `HELLO` of a sender (a `/reload`) is held and handled when its minute is over, not dropped. The churn budget of the busiest client in the 50-client test (SPEC-02 §4) goes from 1.5 to 2 KB/min: it measured 1.0-1.4 KB/min over other seeds and 1.6 KB/min with the test's seed on Linux CI (SPEC-10 R10). Forever full names with a space ("Name Surname-Realm"): one `Compat.SplitName` (the realm after the last hyphen) in the store, the transport, the roster and the bank inbox; `main:` / `alt:` notes match a 3-, 2- or 1-word roster name; `/accord debug` hides each word of such a name; whispers and party invitations no longer refuse it (a whisper goes through `ChatFrame_SendTellWithMessage`, as "/w Name Surname" would whisper "Name"); table cells cut a long name with dots on one line and the row's tooltip shows it whole. `Tests/test_hardening.lua` (every case of §6.1-§6.3, the block summary between real clients, names with a space through every data set) and the 1000-login scenario of §6.5 in the simulator (lean clients: `Sim.new{ lean, loginWithin, ready, names }`).
- M7.5: personal backup ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §4). A **Backup** card on the Settings page (and `/accord backup`) exports the player's own data, the characters, the main of each realm, the account settings, the own `accounts`, `contacts` and `recipes` records of every known guild and the account token, to one line `AZACCORD1:<checksum>:<Base64>` in a read-only box, already selected, under the warning that it holds the token. Import pastes it on another computer and checks it fully before changing anything (header, format version, SHA-256 checksum, Base64/Deflate/CBOR, the shape of every part with the rules of `Config` and the owner rules of `Store`, and whether it belongs to another account), shows a preview of what changes (token kept or replaced, characters added, main, each setting old → new, own records restored or kept because the computer has a newer one: a record is never replaced by an older version) and applies only after *Import*. It writes only `AzerothAccordDB`, never the outbox, then rebuilds the accounts and publishes the own records again. Other players' data, co-play, the bank, the likes and the guild settings are never in it; a damaged, truncated, foreign, newer or other-account text is refused with its message and nothing changes; the text and the token are never printed. `Data/Backup.lua`, `UI/BackupBox.lua`, `Store.ValidShape`; English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_backup.lua`; the wiki (Settings, Commands) in both languages.
- M7.4: demo mode ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §3). `/accord demo` fills every view (Roster, member card, professions and recipes, likes and the group map, bank with logs and requests, Raid, Settings) from an invented guild, "Demo Guild", of 60 members with invented names (two with a space), 20 accounts with the addon, alts in another guild and in none, three professions, 40 likes, 30 days of co-play and the bank with its six kinds of request. The data is one module, `Data/Demo.lua`, a pure deterministic generator the mockup tools of ADR 0019 can load later. English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_demo.lua`; the wiki (Commands) in both languages.
- M7.3: the guild of alts outside the guild ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §2). The `chars` entries of the `accounts` record gain `guild` (the guild's name, `""` for no guild) and `seen` (the day of the alt's last login with the addon) for characters outside the guild, read from the local `AzerothAccordDB.chars` (which now keeps `guildAt`, when a login last read the guild; a character with no guild is registered at login too). ### Addon
