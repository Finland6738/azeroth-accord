### Added

- M8.7: bank requests from the bank contents, with a tip ([SPEC-08](docs/specs/SPEC-08-guild-bank.md) §9-10, added by the owner on 2026-11-25). A **Withdraw** picks its item from the bank tabs the member can see (search by name, the quantity available shown and capping the request, the age of the snapshot as a provenance label; a link or an item number still works) and may promise a **tip** in whole gold, up to 10,000: a donation the member deposits in the bank, never moved by the addon. The officers' **Inbox** shows date and time, requester, item, quantity and tip, sorted by oldest (default), newest or highest tip, and says **Looks paid** when the money log shows a matching deposit. The tip is shown to officers, the Guild Master and the requester only (display rule, SPEC-10 Q16). Older versions ignore the new `tip` field and keep it.
- M8.6: exports for officers ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §6). An **Export** button on the **Roster** view and on the **Inactive** and **Timeline** pages copies out what the page shows, with its filters: **CSV** (fixed English columns, RFC 4180 quoting, an apostrophe before a text a spreadsheet would run as a formula, UTC dates) or **Discord** text (code blocks with aligned columns, parts of at most 1900 characters). The text is in a read-only box already selected, under the warning that it leaves the game; over 256 KB only whole rows are kept and the box says how many are left out. Officers and the Guild Master only; never tokens, GUIDs, the guild key, the game's officer notes or the extended notes. `Data/Export.lua`, `UI/ExportBox.lua`; the multi-line field of the backup box is now `CopyBox.Area`, used by both. `Tests/test_export.lua` reads every CSV back with a strict reader (`Tests/lib/csv.lua`).
- M8.5: extended officer notes ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §5). Officers and the Guild Master get an **Officer notes** box on the member card: one note per character, up to 500 characters, 1000 bytes and 20 lines (a multi-line box with a count; a longer one is refused with *Too long: n / 1000 bytes.*), with who wrote the latest version and when, the other characters' notes of the account read-only below, *Edit the note…* and *Delete the note* (an empty revision, so it reaches every officer; it expires after 60 days). A note is never deleted because its character left the guild (SPEC-10 Q14): the box says *Left the guild on <date>*. New restricted set `onotes` (`Data/OfficerNotes.lua`, `UI/OfficerNotes.lua`): written and accepted from officers only, summarised in 32 groups in the `m` field of `OHELLO`, sent on `OFFICER` once the channel is proven and kept on the computer until then, never on `GUILD` or by whisper to a member, never in an export or the personal backup; a demoted officer's client deletes them with the other officer data and gets them back from the other officers when promoted again. A book icon marks the players with a note on the Inactive page, and step 2 of the cleanup review shows the account's note on one line. Demo mode shows five notes; *What I share* counts the notes you wrote (never their text). English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_officer_notes.lua` (limits, former member, member card, the simulated wire with and without the officer channel, demotion and re-promotion); the wiki (Guild roster, Privacy and data) in both languages.
- M8.4: the cleanup review and the manual-ops queue ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §4). **Review…** on the Inactive page proposes the accounts by inactivity tier (never the ones away or with an officer character; a kept account is not proposed again for 30 days), the officer chooses keep, remove, demote, promote or edit note per account or per character, and the choices fill a **Queue** page with the name to copy and the command to type or the note to paste. Accord never changes the roster, and a static test fails if the addon names a function that does. An entry is ticked when a complete roster read shows it done, expires after 14 days, stays 7 days once closed; at most 200 open ones, local to the officer's computer.
- M8.3: inactivity per account and declared absences ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §3, §8). Officers get an **Officers** tab with two pages. **Inactive** lists the accounts (not the characters) not seen for 14 / 30 / 60 / 90 days or more, from the most recent of all their characters (online or the roster's last online, the `seen` day of an alt outside the guild, the own logins), with the unknown ones last, filters (lowest tier, *Show away*, *Show unknown*), a search and, on a row, *Set absence…* and the member card; **Tiers…** changes the four thresholds for all officers (set `ocfg`). **Timeline** lists the observed changes of the guild by kind, period (7, 30, 90, 365 days) and name, and *Show all* of the member card opens it on that account. A **declared absence** is a return date with no reason (set `absence`, `<GUID>:self` by the member in the new **Absence** card of Settings or with `/accord away`, `<GUID>:off` by an officer from the member card or the page; the most recent write counts for the account; at most 180 days ahead, deleted 30 days after the return): it hides the account until that day, then the list says *away until* for 14 days. A member's declaration goes by whisper to up to two officers online (again after the next login when none was), the only officer-tool record a member sends; officers pass it on `OFFICER` once the channel is proven. A member sees their own declaration, never a tier; a demoted officer keeps only their own account's declarations. Demo mode shows three absences; "What I share" gets *Absence*; `/accord debug` gets *Inactivity* and *Absence*. English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_inactivity.lua`; the wiki (Guild roster, Settings, Commands, Interface tour, Privacy and data) in both languages.
- M8.2: the observed timeline ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md) §2). An officer's addon keeps a snapshot of the roster and compares each complete read with it; a join, a leave, a rank, a level or a public note change becomes an event with its interval (the last time the old value was seen, the first time the new one was) and its observer. The first read only takes the picture; an incomplete read, or a departure or rank change of more than 10 members and a quarter of the guild that a second read 120 s later does not confirm, changes nothing. Events wait 60-300 s (kept across a `/reload`), then go into the observer's own records of the new restricted set `timeline` (one record per observer, day and part: at most 60 events and 8 KB, four parts a day; one level event per character and day); an event another observer already wrote is only counted. When read, the same change seen by two officers (same kind, character and values, intervals overlapping within 300 s) is one event with the narrowest interval and every observer. Records are deleted 365 days after their day. Officers see a **Timeline** box on the member card (the ten latest changes of every character of the account, *Show all*, *Member since*, the state of the officer channel; tooltip with the observers by name or *a former officer*, never a GUID); members get nothing (`Officer.CanSee`, `Officer.VISIBLE_TO`: the proposed answer to SPEC-10 Q13, one table). The records travel only on `OFFICER` or by whisper to an officer (`officerOnly`, checked again when the message leaves the queue, never saved in the outbox, never accepted from `GUILD`), and only once the client has seen an addon message arrive on `OFFICER` in the session: before that they stay on the computer, no whisper fallback; their summary is the new `m` field of `OHELLO`, by month (`Sync:Groups("officer")`), compared by the designated officer, and a record goes out at most every 10 minutes. A demoted officer (no officer character of the account in the guild) loses the officer data and the snapshot. Demo mode shows 40 days of timeline. `/accord debug` gets *Officer tools* and *Timeline*; "What I share" lists the officer tools. English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_timeline.lua`; the wiki (Guild roster, Privacy and data) in both languages.
- M7.6: the **What I share** page of Settings ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §5). A page between Settings and Info lists every data set this character publishes (the characters with their guild and last login, BattleTag and Discord, recipes, time played together, bank snapshots, bank requests, likes, the sync summary, and for officers their own records), who receives it, when it goes out and its current content, read from the records the client last sent (`UI/ShareModel.lua`, `UI/SharePanel.lua`). A single switch, **Record time played together** (`account.settings.shareCoplay`, on by default, schema 3 migration): off, this client neither counts nor publishes co-play and writes no new records; the records already published stay until they expire (90 days). Answers SPEC-10 Q12 (owner, 2026-11-24).
- M7.7: hardening against bad peers and very large guilds ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §6). The codec drops a decoded table deeper than 8 levels (`depth`) and reports how many bytes it decoded; a `REC` with more than 100 records is dropped whole; per sender, one `HELLO`/`BHELLO` a minute and 10 requests of every set a minute are handled, and a sender that makes the client decode more than 256 KB in a minute or sends more than 50 refused records in 10 minutes is in quarantine for 10 minutes (its messages are dropped before decoding; `/accord debug` counts the senders in quarantine, never names them; the counters start again at a change of guild). Above 200 authors a client sends the block summary `BHELLO` (96 blocks of 3 bytes, about 440 B; an author's block is a Fibonacci hash of its key, because the high bits of `Hash.Fast` are nearly the same for the characters of one realm: SPEC-11 §6.4 said 128 blocks from the high bits, changed after the simulator measured both) instead of the per-author `HELLO`, once every officer heard runs a version that knows it; only the designated officer (and a character of the sender's own account) answers with a `DIGEST` by whisper, at most one every 2 s; the first `HELLO` after login waits 10 + random(0, 20 + authors / 10) seconds. The per-author summary is now kept record by record (only the authors of a changed record are hashed again; it is read whole before each `HELLO` sent and after an import, the demo or cleared data), and a `BHELLO` in step with the client is recognised by comparing one string. An officer counts for the choice between `HELLO` and `BHELLO` only once its own `HELLO` has told its version (heard first through a `REC`, as after a server restart, it made nearly every member fall back to the 11 KB per-author `HELLO`). An early `HELLO` of a sender (a `/reload`) is held and handled when its minute is over, not dropped. The churn budget of the busiest client in the 50-client test (SPEC-02 §4) goes from 1.5 to 2 KB/min: it measured 1.0-1.4 KB/min over other seeds and 1.6 KB/min with the test's seed on Linux CI (SPEC-10 R10). Forever full names with a space ("Name Surname-Realm"): one `Compat.SplitName` (the realm after the last hyphen) in the store, the transport, the roster and the bank inbox; `main:` / `alt:` notes match a 3-, 2- or 1-word roster name; `/accord debug` hides each word of such a name; whispers and party invitations no longer refuse it (a whisper goes through `ChatFrame_SendTellWithMessage`, as "/w Name Surname" would whisper "Name"); table cells cut a long name with dots on one line and the row's tooltip shows it whole. `Tests/test_hardening.lua` (every case of §6.1-§6.3, the block summary between real clients, names with a space through every data set) and the 1000-login scenario of §6.5 in the simulator (lean clients: `Sim.new{ lean, loginWithin, ready, names }`).
- M7.5: personal backup ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §4). A **Backup** card on the Settings page (and `/accord backup`) exports the player's own data, the characters, the main of each realm, the account settings, the own `accounts`, `contacts` and `recipes` records of every known guild and the account token, to one line `AZACCORD1:<checksum>:<Base64>` in a read-only box, already selected, under the warning that it holds the token. Import pastes it on another computer and checks it fully before changing anything (header, format version, SHA-256 checksum, Base64/Deflate/CBOR, the shape of every part with the rules of `Config` and the owner rules of `Store`, and whether it belongs to another account), shows a preview of what changes (token kept or replaced, characters added, main, each setting old → new, own records restored or kept because the computer has a newer one: a record is never replaced by an older version) and applies only after *Import*. It writes only `AzerothAccordDB`, never the outbox, then rebuilds the accounts and publishes the own records again. Other players' data, co-play, the bank, the likes and the guild settings are never in it; a damaged, truncated, foreign, newer or other-account text is refused with its message and nothing changes; the text and the token are never printed. `Data/Backup.lua`, `UI/BackupBox.lua`, `Store.ValidShape`; English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_backup.lua`; the wiki (Settings, Commands) in both languages.
- M7.4: demo mode ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §3). `/accord demo` fills every view (Roster, member card, professions and recipes, likes and the group map, bank with logs and requests, Raid, Settings) from an invented guild, "Demo Guild", of 60 members with invented names (two with a space), 20 accounts with the addon, alts in another guild and in none, three professions, 40 likes, 30 days of co-play and the bank with its six kinds of request. It works without a guild and without a connection, sends nothing (`Transport:Send` refuses with the reason `demo`, the waiting queue stays and goes out after the exit, what arrives is dropped and counted as `demo` in `/accord debug`, Sync starts no timer) and saves nothing (`Config.db` points at deep copies for the whole demo; the saved variables are never touched). A "Demo" strip with *Exit demo* sits above the tabs, and the title bar says *Demo*. `Compat:SetOverlay` / `ClearOverlay` make the character, the guild and the roster answer from the invented guild without calling the game. The data is one module, `Data/Demo.lua`, a pure deterministic generator the mockup tools of ADR 0019 can load later. English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_demo.lua`; the wiki (Commands) in both languages.
- M7.3: the guild of alts outside the guild ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §2). The `chars` entries of the `accounts` record gain `guild` (the guild's name, `""` for no guild) and `seen` (the day of the alt's last login with the addon) for characters outside the guild, read from the local `AzerothAccordDB.chars` (which now keeps `guildAt`, when a login last read the guild; a character with no guild is registered at login too). The member card, the grouped Roster rows and their tooltip say *Other guild: <name> · seen 3 d ago* or *No guild · seen today* instead of *not in guild*, with the M7.2 label (shared, dimmed after 30 days). When the list does not fit, the characters of the guild stay before the ones outside, the most recent login first; older clients ignore both fields and the record stays under 4096 B. Characters known only from a note show no guild. English and Italian strings; tests in `Tests/test_accounts.lua`, the card, the model and `Tests/test_provenance.lua`; the wiki (Guild Roster, Privacy and data) in both languages.
- M7.2: provenance labels ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md) §1). One widget, `Widgets.Provenance`, shows the source of a value (`game`, an age, `you`, `note`) with a tooltip that names who reported it; a shared value older than its threshold (`Provenance.DIM_DAYS`) is dimmed. Used by the member card (professions, contacts, alts, addon version, likes), the Roster profession tooltips, the recipe list header and the guild bank tabs. `Provenance.AgeText` is now the single age scale (it adds months and years to `Shell.Ago`). English and Italian strings; `Tests/test_provenance.lua`.

### Changed

- The owner answered SPEC-10 Q13-Q15 (2026-11-24). Q13 and Q15 as proposed: the timeline, inactivity, the action queue, extended notes and the exports are for officers and the GM only; a member sees their own absence (a return date, no reason) but not their inactivity tier; any officer chooses the tiers for all officers; exports carry names, ranks, levels, last online, tiers, absences, the public note and the timeline, never the game's officer note, extended notes, tokens or GUIDs, with a warning above the box. **Q14 changed:** the observed timeline is **kept forever** with the old and new text of public notes (the 365-day retention of M8.2 is gone: `Store` no longer expires `timeline` records and `gone` is kept), and so are the extended notes of people who left the guild (M8.5); a demoted officer cannot see what they saw as an officer, so their client still deletes the officer data locally (a file left on disk could be read outside the game) and the history returns from the other officers if they are promoted again. No age cap is invented: only the per-event and per-record size limits apply; the endless growth is the new risk SPEC-10 R12 (to be measured), and SPEC-12 §1.5 describes a transfer limit (three months per answer, newest first) for a new officer's catch-up, not yet built. `Tests/test_timeline.lua`: records of any age are kept, an old record is accepted from an officer, nothing but the demotion removes them.

### Design

- M8.1: specification of M8, officer tools ([SPEC-12](docs/specs/SPEC-12-officer-tools.md), Italian; documentation only). Who sees what (officers and the GM see everything; a member sees only their own declared absence) and four restricted data sets, `timeline`, `absence`, `onotes` and `ocfg`, that travel only on `OFFICER` or by whisper to an officer, checked again when the message leaves the queue, and stay on the officer's computer until the officer channel (S5) is proven in the session, with no whisper fallback; their summary is a new `m` field of `OHELLO`, by month of the timeline and by note bucket, so older officer clients see nothing new; a demoted officer's account deletes them. The observed timeline (join, leave, rank, level, public note) from a roster snapshot kept by officers, with a baseline read, complete-read and mass-change guards, events with an interval `[a, b]`, one record per observer and day (60 events, up to 4 parts), level-ups grouped per day, two observers merged when kind, member and values match and their intervals overlap within 300 s, 365-day retention. Inactivity per account from the most recent of its characters (native last online, the `seen` day of alts outside the guild, the own account), tiers 14 / 30 / 60 / 90 days set by any officer, declared absences (`:self` from the member's account by whisper to up to two online officers, `:off` from an officer, at most 180 days). The cleanup review fills a local queue of manual actions (promote, demote, remove, edit note) with the game command to type; the addon calls no protected roster function (a static test lists them); queued actions tick from the roster, expire after 14 days and are cleared 7 days after closing. Extended notes of up to 500 characters / 1000 bytes per character. CSV (RFC 4180, formula-safe) and Discord code-block exports of the roster, inactive accounts and the timeline, never with tokens, GUIDs or officer notes. [ADR 0023](docs/adr/0023-officer-tools-observe-and-suggest-officer-data-stays-among-officers.md); SPEC-00 §5, SPEC-02 §1-§3 and §6, SPEC-03 §9 updated; SPEC-10 gains R11 (no last-online API) and the open questions Q13 (visibility), Q14 (retention of notes about former members) and Q15 (what exports contain), each answered by a proposal the steps follow until the owner decides.
- M7.1: specification of M7, data trust ([SPEC-11](docs/specs/SPEC-11-data-trust.md), Italian; documentation only). Provenance labels (`native`, `shared`, `own`, `note`), the age scale and the days after which old shared values are dimmed (bank 7, recipes 60, an alt's other guild 30, addon version 30, contacts 180); the guild of alts outside the guild as two optional fields of `chars` entries (`guild`, `seen` rounded to the day), kept inside the 4096-byte limit that older clients enforce, with roster characters kept first; demo mode (`/accord demo`, an overlay over Compat, copies of the saved tables, a deterministic invented guild reusable by the mockup tools of ADR 0019); the personal backup (`AZACCORD1:<checksum>:<Base64>`, what is and is never included, six refusal checks, a preview); the "What I share" page; hardening limits (100 records per `REC`, per-sender flood limits and a 10-minute quarantine, a block summary `BHELLO` above 200 authors, the 1000-login burst budget, names with spaces). SPEC-02 §4, SPEC-03 §1, SPEC-05 ACC-03 and ADR 0006 (the owner's decision of 2026-11-20: the guild of each listed character is shared) updated; SPEC-10 gains R8, R9 and the open question Q12 (a switch for co-play).
