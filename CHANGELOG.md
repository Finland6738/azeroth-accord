# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Design

- Specification of the first version in `docs/specs/` (SPEC-00 to SPEC-10, in Italian): scope, roles and the owner's decisions; platform and architecture; guild-only network protocol and trust rules; data model; the Accord tab in the guild window; extended roster, alts and contacts; professions and recipes; likes and the social index; guild bank; quality, spike and release; risks and open questions.
- Architecture decisions 0001–0018 in `docs/adr/`.
- Decisions confirmed by the owner: likes from account to account, irreversible and anonymous (only totals); alt list, BattleTag and Discord always shared with the guild; milestone order roster → professions → reputation → bank; name Azeroth Accord, GPL-3.0.

### Planning

- Development roadmap in `docs/roadmap/`: M0 spike to M6 release (50 steps) with branch, dependencies, scope, acceptance criteria, tests, docs and real-client checks, plus the V2 backlog.
- Milestones M7 to M15 (63 steps) after the owner's review of a guild-addon specification: data trust and quality (with the guild of alts outside the guild), officer tools and roster history, the Action Center and notifications, calendar, raids and the guild's season roster (MS1 / MS2 / OS, attendance optional and off by default), loot and the guild handbook (editable pages; loot documented with its addon, no loot engine), recruitment and trials, economy, board and polls, community. The V2 backlog now maps each old idea to its milestone and lists what stays out.
- M7.8 planned: an About page in the addon and a Credits section in the READMEs and the wiki (created and designed by Finland, for the Italian guild NEBULA); M7's check becomes M7.9.
- One version per milestone ([ADR 0020](docs/adr/0020-one-version-per-milestone.md)): `v0.<milestone>.0`, fixes raise the last number, `v1.0.0` after M15. M5 becomes `v0.5.0` (was the `v0.9.0` beta) and M6 `v0.6.0` (was `v1.0.0`).

- M3.1: `Data/Recipes.lua`, the native recipe source (SPEC-06 §1, ADR 0010): the game's guild recipe directory (`C_GuildInfo.QueryGuildMemberRecipes`, `GUILD_RECIPE_KNOWN_BY_MEMBERS`, `GetGuildMemberRecipes`) behind the `guildRecipesNative` capability check, one query at a time (queue of 20 without duplicates, 2 seconds apart, 8-second timeout, a rest after 5 unanswered queries), answers cached in memory for 15 minutes and read as a list or a set of spell ids; a bitfield answer is recognised and switches the source off. The shared lists (M3.2 / M3.3) are a complete fallback that works alone; the choice between the sources waits for the M0.4 in-game results. `Compat.TradeSkill.GuildMemberRecipes` now passes the member and the skill line and returns every value.
- M3.2: `Data/RecipeScan.lua` and the list encoding in `Data/Recipes.lua` (SPEC-06 §1, §3, SPEC-03 §3, PRO-06): when a profession window opens or changes the own recipes are read (`GetBaseProfessionInfo`, `GetAllRecipeIDs` or the filtered list, `GetRecipeInfo(id).learned`), encoded as sorted spell ids in delta varints and Base64 (about 1.5 bytes per recipe before Base64), hashed with the skill rank, and published as one `recipes` record per character and profession when the hash changes, at most once every 10 minutes per profession (a change in the pause waits and goes out with the latest state). A filtered list can only add recipes, never remove them. The decoder refuses malformed text. The store refuses a `recipes` record whose id is not `<GUID>:<skill line>`; Cooking, First Aid and Fishing are published but are not profession cells in the roster.
- M3.3: recipe sync (SPEC-06 §1.4, SPEC-02 §4.7): the `recipes` set travels with the normal per-author sync, and `Recipes:Lookup` now reads one place for every view: the native answer first (with the age of the shared list too), else the member's shared list (the own scan for the player), decoded once per revision; `Recipes:ProfessionsOf`; `RECIPES_UPDATED` on the Bus for native and shared arrivals. Simulator: a 400-recipe list reaches 20 clients in under 10 s with about 1.3 KB sent in all, a late login stays inside the 8 KB login budget, an unchanged list costs nothing.

- M4.1: `Data/Notary.lua`, the guild salt of the likes and the choice of the notary (SPEC-07 §2.1, LIKE-05..LIKE-07, ADR 0007 note): officers ask for the salt on `OFFICER` after login and share what another officer lacks; members never hold it, a demoted officer drops it, a salt from a sender who is not an officer is ignored. While `OFFICER` has not been seen carrying addon messages (S5), `SALT` messages also go by whisper to the officers heard recently. An officer who gets no answer creates the salt (`Hash.RandomBytes`, an SHA-256 entropy pool, because the client has no secure random source); salts created at the same time are all kept (at most eight) and every officer uses the oldest. The voter's notary is itself when it is an officer holding a salt, else an online officer ranked by a hash of the two GUIDs. `Config.Base64Decode`, `Sync:Peers`, RFC 4231 test case 5, the simulator options `notary` and `officerChannel`.
- M4.2: `Data/Likes.lua`, votes and the ledger (SPEC-07 §2.2, LIKE-01..LIKE-09, ADR 0007 note): the voter's queue and the accounts it liked stay on its computer (shared by all its characters); votes go by whisper (`VOTE`) to one online officer, wait as pending while none is online, try the next officer when one cannot notarize or does not answer, and settle on the officer's receipt (`LEDGER` by whisper with the new entry). The notary checks guild membership, the voter's account, self and alt votes, a token copied by another account (`conflict`) and a double vote (pseudonyms of every token and tags of every character of the voter, under every salt of the ring), then writes `{pseudonym, tag, target, day}`: the pseudonym is HMAC-SHA256(salt, token) truncated to 128 bits. The ledger reaches the guild through the normal sync; the store keeps it append-only (rev 1, id derived from the content, never replaced) and accepts entries from officers even when their notary is no longer one. Totals count distinct pseudonyms over the proved characters of an account, so alts linked later merge without counting a voter twice; members without the addon receive likes on their character. The roster's Likes column becomes available (not shown by default). Simulator option `likes` (with it every simulated client starts with its own account token).
- M4.3: the like interface (SPEC-07 §2.3, LIKE-01, LIKE-04, LIKE-05, LIKE-08): `UI/Reputation.lua`, the **Reputation** view (`/accord reputation`), one row per account of the guild with its main, number of characters, place, total and a **Like** button that needs a second click to confirm (a like cannot be taken back), then *pending* or *liked*; sort by likes or name, search by any character, a click opens the member card. The view has modes for the next steps.
- M4.4: `Data/Social.lua`, the social index (SPEC-07 §3.2, SOC-04, SOC-06, ADR 0008 note): over the last 30 realm days the maximum over observers per day and pair, characters merged into accounts (proved characters only, pairs inside one account left out), minutes counted per partner, effective partners `N = exp(entropy)`, `A` = accounts with at least an hour, `index = min(100, round(100·N/(A−1)))`, *not enough data* under 5 hours; top five partners with their shares; computed locally in coroutine steps (one per frame), at most once every ten minutes when the data changed, first 90 s after login. The three worked examples of SPEC-07 §3.2 are numeric tests (10, 22, 75). `Accounts:ProvedChars` and `Accounts:AccountKey` are shared by the likes and the index. Nothing is shown yet (M4.5).

- M5.5: bank requests (SPEC-08 BANK-20..BANK-22, §9; SPEC-03 §7; ADR 0009 rule 4): `Data/BankRequests.lua` and the **Requests** mode of the Bank view. A member opens a withdrawal, deposit or donation request (an item link or number, or gold; a reason of up to 120 characters; five open requests at most) and cancels it while it is open; only officers move it to approved, rejected (with a reason) or fulfilled, with a history; every guildmate sees every request. The store checks it on every client: ids start with the requester's GUID, decisions need an officer as sender and author, the content never changes, and when two versions meet the highest status wins, so a decision and a cancel written at the same time end the same everywhere. Offline tests, with the network simulator: `Tests/test_bank_requests.lua`.
