# M12 in-game check: recruitment, trials, welcome, mentors, the Material 3 look and the fixes since v0.11.0

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M12** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has checked M12 on WoW Forever. When it passes, the second M12.9 box is ticked and `v0.12.0` counts as checked ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)). (The checks of the earlier versions, [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md), [the release check](release.md), [M7](M7.md), [M8](M8.md), [M9](M9.md), [M10](M10.md) and [M11](M11.md), are open too: run those first, or all together. Where they describe the old look of tabs, buttons, chips or dialogs, step 7 of this list is the one that counts. Where they open a member's own recipe list, it is switched off for now: see step 8.)

**Exit criterion of M12:**

- an officer copies a recruitment text built from the season gaps;
- a trial member shows in the Action Center when the trial ends;
- a new member sees a welcome checklist.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M12.9 box in [M12-recruitment.md](../roadmap/M12-recruitment.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)); the Action Center of M9 ([the M9 check](M9.md)); a season and a target composition of M10 ([the M10 check](M10.md)); the handbook of M11 ([the M11 check](M11.md)).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**:
  - **O**, an officer (the Guild Master helps), and a second officer **P**;
  - **A**, a member who was in the guild before; one account that also logs a **second character**;
  - **N**, a character that **joins the guild during the check**, from an account that has no other character in it, with Accord installed before it joins; if you can, a second one, **N2**, that installs Accord **after** joining.
- Discord, to paste a text.
- A copy of the saved variables made with `v0.11.0`, if you have one (see step 1).
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).
- **Close the game completely and start it again** after installing: the new pictures of the look are not picked up by `/reload`.

`/accord demo` has invented data for M12 (a season with gaps, a *Guild info* page, three trials with notes, a welcome checklist, five mentor offers): use it for the **look** (steps 2 to 7) when you are alone. Nothing it shows proves anything about the game or about other players.

## 1. Upgrade from v0.11.0

`v0.11.0` **did not start** on WoW Forever on a first install (the client has no `math.randomseed`), so you may have no saved data of it. If you have some (or saved data of a development build made after it):

1. Install this version, restart the game: **no Lua error**; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`, schema **4**.
2. Settings, roster, recipes, likes, bank, season, events, availability, notices, groups and handbook pages are all still there. Your characters are listed together, none filed under a surname as if it were a realm.
3. New: the officers' **Recruitment text** button and **Trials** page, the **Mentors** mode in Reputation, `/accord help` lists `recruit` and `welcome`, *What I share* has **Mentor offer** and **Welcome checklist**.
4. Who was already in the guild gets **no** welcome checklist (card, chat line or Action Center row).

On every client, also without old data:

5. A first install (no saved variables of Accord) starts: `/accord`, the Accord tab and the game's options panel are there, no error.
6. If a client can still run `v0.11.0`: keep it online with the new ones for half an hour after trials, offers and recipes have been written: no error there or here, nothing of M12 on it, and `/accord debug` → *Sync* and *Store* on both show **no endless repair** and nobody ignored (question 11).

## 2. Recruitment text (M12.2, exit criterion)

1. **O** has an open season with a target composition that has gaps, and a *Guild info* page with a first paragraph (with a link and accented letters in it).
2. **A** (not an officer): no button in **Season**, and `/accord recruit` says the text is for officers.
3. **Exit:** **O** presses **Recruitment text** (or types `/accord recruit`): guild name, faction and realm, the description (links shown as their text), *Looking for:* with the roles missing by MS1, the contact. Ctrl+C copies the selected text.
4. **Chat line**: paste it in the chat box and send it yourself in a channel: it fits whole, at most 255 bytes, with the description shortened first. **Discord**: paste it in Discord (question 1).
5. Change **Contact** and **Extra line** (try the longest, with accents): the text follows; both are still there after a `/reload`; **P** does not see them.
6. A member declares, O changes the target or the page, or the source becomes FDCP: the open window writes the text again. Without an open season, a target, gaps or a *Guild info* paragraph, the window says which part is missing.
7. **Nothing is ever posted**: open the window with every combination and watch the chat and the channels. No Accord message appears anywhere.

## 3. Trials (M12.3, exit criterion)

1. Only **O** and **P** see the **Trials** page (Officers, after *Timeline*) and the **Trial** section of the member card. **A** and the member on trial see nothing of it, anywhere.
2. **Start trial** from the page and from the card: 14 days and today by default; try 1 and 60 days, a first day 30 days back and 14 ahead, and values outside (refused).
3. **P** receives the trial (question 4; without a working officer channel the page says it stays on this computer).
4. **Extend** moves the end (never more than 120 days from the start). **Passed**, **Not passed** and **Cancel trial** ask **Confirm**; a closed trial cannot be reopened and shows under **Closed (90 days)** and on the card. *Passed* with the promotion switch on adds one entry to the queue; nobody is promoted by the addon.
5. **Notes**: O and P each write one; each can change only their own; the limits of the officer notes apply.
6. **Exit:** start a trial that ends **today** (a start back in time with the right length). The Action Center shows *Trial of … ended on …: decide* and one notice comes; a click opens that trial. After a `/reload` and another login the row stays and the notice does **not** come again. Extend it: the row goes, and a new notice comes on the new end day. Check the date (question 5).
7. The member on trial leaves the guild: the row says *… left the guild during the trial* until an officer cancels it.
8. Switch the trial notice off in **Settings**: no notice, the row stays. Demote P (if possible): P's trials and notes leave P's computer.

## 4. Welcome checklist (M12.4, exit criterion)

1. **N** joins the guild while playing. Within some minutes (the guild's data must arrive first): one chat line, a **Welcome** card on top of the Action Center and a row in its list (question 3).
2. **Exit:** the card lists up to four items with the count. Each click opens the right place; each item **ticks itself**: read *Guild info* and *Rules*, choose the main (already ticked with one character), add the Discord name, declare for the season (*Not sure yet* counts). With FDCP as the source, check the last item too (question 6). An item the guild cannot offer (no pages, no season) is not listed.
3. A ticked item stays ticked when the data changes. With everything done the card says so, and is gone from the next login.
4. **Dismiss** on another new character: gone for good, also after a `/reload`, a logout and *Clear guild data*. `/accord welcome` still opens it, for anyone, also for **A**.
5. **N2** installs Accord after joining: does the card appear? Write down what `/accord debug` → *Welcome* says (sign, *guild log functions* yes or no) (question 2).
6. No checklist for: who was in the guild before; an alt of an account already in the guild; a guild that has just started using Accord.
7. It is **local**: O sees nothing of N's progress. After 30 days it goes by itself (check if you can wait or return later).

## 5. Mentors (M12.5)

1. Nobody is listed before asking. **A** opens **Reputation** → **Mentors** and presses **Offer to mentor new members**: within seconds everybody sees A. **Edit**: topics and a note (80 bytes, accents).
2. The topic filter and the search work; **Whisper** only opens the chat box (question 8). Nothing is sent by the addon.
3. The same offer from the other character of A's account is refused or edits the existing one: one offer per account.
4. **Leave the mentor list**, also with the switch on the *Mentor offer* card of *What I share*: A disappears for everybody, also for a member who was offline and logs in later.
5. **Only officers** see **Suggested mentors**: the third tab of the **Trials** page and a section on a new member's card, for members on trial or joined in the last 30 days, with up to three of those who offered and why, and **Whisper** for both. A member who did not offer never appears. After an evening of dungeons with a new member the reasons make sense (question 7).
6. **No notice** reaches the mentor or the new member; nobody who is not an officer sees a pair.

## 6. The new screens (M12), look

Open the recruitment window, the *Trials* page and its dialogs, the trial section and note box, the *Welcome* card and window, the *Mentors* mode and offer dialog and the suggestions, in **Nebula, Aurora and Accord**, in **English and Italian**, in the guild window and in the Accord window (also at 720 × 460). Nothing is cut, overlaps or leaves its window (question 9).

## 7. The Material 3 look (M12.6 to M12.8)

Go through **every view and page** (Action Center, Roster, Professions, Reputation, Bank and its pages, Groups, Season, Events with Rallies, Availability, Raid, Handbook with editor and revisions, Guild addons, Officers, Settings, the member card, `/accord about`, `/accord debug`) in the **three themes**:

1. **Tabs**: 48 high, each with its icon; the open one has name and icon in the accent's colour and a short rounded bar under them, a line under the whole bar; no box, tint or glow (this replaces the boxed tabs you saw before: say if you want them back). The bar slides; **More** holds the tabs without room; counts are red badges.
2. **Buttons**: at most **one filled** button in a view or window, the others softly filled, outlined or plain text; icon buttons (close, the handbook's arrows) are round on hover. No button is taller than its row.
3. **Chips** (filters): off is outlined; on is filled **with a check mark** and grows; rows of chips wrap without covering anything (Roster columns, mentor topics, group kinds, long Italian labels). **Segmented buttons** (theme, font, pages of a view): the chosen one is filled with a check.
4. **States**: hover shades a control lightly (darker on Aurora, never white); a disabled control is faded; a click works on the whole shape.
5. **Selected row**: the same soft fill in Roster, Groups, Guild addons, Handbook and revisions, the request opened from the Action Center, the line-up. Tables sort and scroll as before; text stays readable in every row.
6. **Menus, fields and multi-line boxes**: menus open in the right place and fit the screen; the focused field has a thick accent outline.
7. **Switches** (Settings, *What I share*), **progress bars**, **badges** and the guild window's **side tabs** (the open one softly filled, the count a red badge).
8. **Member card**: a side sheet beside the Accord window, on the left when the screen has no room; it follows the window and scrolls.
9. **Dialogs**: title at the top, buttons bottom right, the dim behind, Esc closes; the **cleanup review** fits your screen with the interface scale you use.
10. **Tooltips**: a short one is a small light-on-dark (dark-on-light in Aurora) box, a long one a larger panel with a title; none covers what you point at or leaves the screen. The **toast** of a notice is readable.
11. The standalone window opens at 1380 × 720 (smaller on a small screen); text and icons are sharp, no missing corners.

## 8. Fixes since v0.11.0

(Those the [M11 check](M11.md) does not cover.)

1. **Start:** see step 1.5.
2. **Accord tab:** under the lowest tab of the guild window, covering none, in the game's tab frame with the Accord logo. A click opens the **Accord window** and closes the guild window; with *Open Accord inside the guild window* on, the panel opens inside it, covers the community list and cuts what does not fit.
3. **Tables fit** the window, also narrowed: no column title outside, texts cut with dots. The Roster shows **BattleTag** (yours from the game, others' as shared), no *Contacts* or *Social* column; rank, zone and the other columns are readable.
4. **Member card:** opens as a window beside the Accord window and the table keeps its width.
5. **Likes:** long names stay on one line; the **heart** gives a like and asks to confirm.
6. **Guild, characters and sending:** `/accord debug` says the guild key is known; your second character is listed with the first; a change on one client reaches the others within minutes, also if the debug report says the client calls itself restricted.
7. **Profession levels:** your own match the game's Skills panel and update on a level-up; others show a real level or only the profession's name, never a wrong "1".
8. **Recipe catalogue:** the **Professions** tab opens the guild's recipes on *Somebody can craft it*; the first member with a profession who opens its window shares the **full** list; a recipe lists who can craft it with **Request craft** (only a whisper written for you); the tooltip sits next to the name. A click on a member's profession (table, card, craft reminder) opens the guild's recipes of it; a crafter's row opens nothing. A linked or guild-view profession window is not read. Write down the *RecipeScan* lines of `/accord debug` (is there a required-level field?).
9. **Guild bank:** opening the vault reads it (`/accord debug` → *Bank* says which event). **Log**, **Donations** and **Money** are tables whose titles sort (second click reverses), the periods are chips, *Money* shows the chart and the money log, and *Items* has its header; each page sits in one card.

## 9. Taint, saved data, traffic and size

1. Use every new window, page, list and button in and out of combat, then the guild window and the game: no "action blocked", no Lua error. A dialog or notice does not get in the way during a boss fight.
2. `/reload` and a full logout: trials, notes, offers, the checklist and your two recruitment lines come back; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`, and *Store* counts `trials` and `mentor`.
3. On **A**: *Store* shows no trial; *What I share* never shows trial text on any client.
4. *Clear guild data*: offers (and an officer's trials) come back from the others; a dismissed checklist stays dismissed. A backup export and import (M7) gives no error.
5. In a large guild, note in `/accord debug` → *Sync* and *Sending* the traffic the evening the version is installed and some days later.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or "action blocked" | the error text and what you did |
| Something badly laid out or hard to read | a screenshot, the theme, the language, the window and the interface scale |
| Recruitment text wrong or cut | the text you copied, the format and a screenshot of the season's counts |
| Trial or note missing, or seen by a member | who wrote it and when, both clients' ranks; `/accord debug` → *Officer tools*, *Store* and *Sync* |
| Checklist missing, late or shown to the wrong member | when the character joined, when Accord was installed; `/accord debug` → *Welcome* |
| Mentor offer or suggestion wrong | who offered, who is new; `/accord debug` → *Mentors* and *Store* |
| A fix of step 8 does not hold | the step number, `/accord debug` and a screenshot |
| Upgrade problem | `/accord debug` → *Saved data* and the old saved file |
