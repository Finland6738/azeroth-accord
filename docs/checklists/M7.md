# M7 in-game check: data trust (labels, alt guilds, demo, backup, "What I share", hardening)

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M7** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.7.0` is tagged ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)); until then there is no release. (`v0.1.0` to `v0.6.0` wait for their own checks: [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md) and [the release check](release.md). Run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M7:** in game, the member card of a player with an alt in another guild shows "Other guild: <name>" with the date that alt was last seen. The "What I share" panel lists exactly what the player's own records contain.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M7.8 box in [M7-data-trust.md](../roadmap/M7-data-trust.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)).
- **At least three clients with the addon**:
  - **A**, in the guild, with an **alt in another guild** (a second character of the same account, in a guild whose name has **spaces and an accent**, if you can create one);
  - **B**, another member of your guild with the addon (it sees A's data);
  - **C**, optionally a character with **no guild**.
- A character whose name has a **space** ("Name Surname"), if Forever lets you create one.
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).

## 1. Provenance labels (M7.2)

1. `/reload` on every client: **no Lua error**, no chat message at login.
2. Open the **Roster**, a **member card**, a **recipe list** (Professions), the **Reputation** view and the **Bank** view. Each shows a small label with the source and the age of the value.
3. Is the label **readable and well placed**: on the member card, in the recipe header, in the bank info line and in the roster cells (not cut, not on top of other text), on the three themes (Nebula, Aurora, Accord)?
4. **Dimming**: a value that is old (see SPEC-11: 60 days for recipes) looks **dimmer** than a fresh one, and the bank grid looks dimmed when its snapshot is old. Is it still readable?
5. **Tooltip** of the label: it says who reported the value and when. Does it clash with the tooltip of the member card's row?

## 2. Alts in another guild (M7.3, exit criterion)

1. Log **A's alt in the other guild** once (so it records its guild), then log **A** (in your guild).
2. On **B**, open A's **member card**: the alt shows **"Other guild: <name> · seen N days ago"** with the guild name spelled **exactly as in the game**. **This is the exit criterion.**
3. The name with **spaces and accents** is complete and correctly spelled (also in the Roster's grouped rows, where the line is cut in the narrow cell: the row tooltip has the whole line).
4. Log the alt of C (**no guild**): its row says **"No guild"**. Right after login, not "No guild" for a character that has one (question 1).
5. **Change of guild**: move the alt to another guild (or leave its guild), log it in again, then log A in: B shows the new guild after a while.
6. A character known only from a public note shows **no guild**.

## 3. Demo mode (M7.4)

1. `/accord demo` **in a character with no guild** (or `/accord demo` on A): every view opens (Roster, member card, Professions and recipes, Reputation, Bank, Raid, Settings), filled with invented names.
2. The **strip** "Demo: invented guild. Nothing is sent or saved." is readable on the three themes; the **Exit demo** button works; the tab bar still fits in the guild window's panel.
3. The **classes and levels** look right for Forever (ten classes, maximum level). The demo's **spells and items** have real names: a number in place of a name means a wrong id (write it down).
4. In the demo give a like, send a bank request, choose a main and another theme. **Exit the demo**: your real data and settings are **exactly as before**.
5. `/accord demo`, then **`/logout` while still in the demo** and log in again: no demo data in your real records (`/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`).
6. B sees **nothing** from A's demo (no message sent).

## 4. Personal backup (M7.5)

1. `/accord backup` (or Settings → *Backup*) → **Export**: the box opens with the text selected. **Ctrl+C**.
2. Paste into a text file: one line starting with `AZACCORD1:`. Make a **long** one if you can (many characters, many recipes): is it complete?
3. On the same client (or another computer): **Import**, **Ctrl+V** the text, **Check**. Does the box **accept the whole text** (up to 256 KB) without cutting it? How long did the paste and the check take, and did the game freeze?
4. The preview lists what would change; **Import** applies it. The round trip restores the same characters, main, settings and records.
5. Cut a few characters from the text: **Check** says the text is damaged and nothing changes. A text that is not a backup is refused with its message.

## 5. Hardening (M7.7)

1. With **A and B** online together, `/accord debug` on both after a few minutes: *Sync* `refused 0`, no growing queue, no Lua error.
2. If you can, log in with **more clients at once** (or on a busy day with the whole guild): the game does not stutter and no one is disconnected. Note the *Sync* traffic line (messages and bytes) and compare it with SPEC-09 §5.
3. The throttling: does anything look **dropped or slow** (a record that reaches B after several minutes)? Write down how long a full sync takes.

## 6. Names with a space (M7.7)

On a character with a name like "Name Surname" (or on any member who has one):

1. The member is **listed and synced** in the Roster; B sees its records (alts, recipes, contacts). If not, `/accord debug` → *Sync* on both.
2. **Whisper**: the *Request craft* button (Professions) and the whisper button of the member card open a whisper to the **whole name**, not to "Name".
3. **Invite**: the invite button of a co-play suggestion invites that character.
4. A long name is cut with dots in the Roster columns and complete in the tooltip.
5. A **public note** `alt: Name Surname, tank` is read as an alt of that character.
6. `/accord debug` text and screenshots: the name is replaced as a whole.

## 7. "What I share" (M7.6)

1. **Settings → What I share** (between *Settings* and *Info*): cards for the alts and their guilds, BattleTag and Discord, recipes, co-play and bank snapshots. **Compare with what B sees of you** (member card, recipes): the same content.
2. **Layout**: card heights, wrapping of long lines, scrolling with the wheel, on the three themes.
3. The **class** of each character in the alt card is right.
4. Wording "sent when …" matches what the session did (the sync summary interval).
5. **Switch off** *Record time played together* ("Registra il tempo giocato insieme" in Italian): the card says it is off; play in a group for a few minutes: **no new co-play** is recorded or published (`/accord debug` → *Store*); the other members still show the group you were in.
6. **Switch it on again**: recording resumes.

## 8. Taint, combat and saved data

1. Use the new pages in and out of combat: no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` on A and B: everything comes back; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error | the error text (error frame or BugSack) and what you did |
| A wrong or missing guild name for an alt | the name as the game shows it and what the card shows; `/accord debug` |
| A label cut, misplaced or unreadable | a screenshot (blur names if you like) and the theme |
| The backup text is cut or the game freezes | the length of the text and how long it took |
| A name with a space does not sync, whisper or invite | the name's shape, the button and `/accord debug` *Sync* |
| The "What I share" page differs from what B sees | both screens |
