# M8 in-game check: officer tools (timeline, inactivity, cleanup queue, notes, export, bank requests with a tip)

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M8** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. The owner decided (2026-11-25) to release `v0.8.0` without waiting for it ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)); the check stays open, and what it finds is fixed in a `v0.8.<F>` release. (The checks of the earlier versions, [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md), [the release check](release.md) and [M7](M7.md), are open too: run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M8:** in game, an officer sees a member's joins, rank changes and level-ups on a timeline, and an inactive-accounts list that does not include a player who only plays an alt.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M8.8 box in [M8-officer-tools.md](../roadmap/M8-officer-tools.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)); for step 7, the bank of M5 ([the M5 check](M5.md)).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**:
  - **O**, an officer (a Guild Master helps: promoting, demoting and removing need the rank);
  - **O2**, a second officer, online at the same time as O for the shared parts;
  - **M**, a plain member;
  - **T**, a character you can invite, promote, demote, note and remove (it may be M's alt).
- One player with **two characters**: one not played for 14 days or more (in the guild or listed as an alt outside it) and an alt played now.
- A guild bank with items in at least two tabs, and some gold to deposit.
- Optionally: an older build of the addon on one client (step 7), a spreadsheet and a Discord channel (step 6).
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).

`/accord demo` fills the officer pages with invented data, also on a client that is not an officer: use it to look at layouts when the real guild has nothing to show yet. Nothing is sent or saved, so it proves nothing about the real client's data.

## 1. Observed timeline (M8.2, exit criterion)

1. `/reload` on every client: **no Lua error**. O logs in for the first time with this version: **no event** appears for the members already in the guild (the first complete read is only a picture).
2. `/accord debug` on O, a minute after login and again later → *Timeline*: `snapshot held`, `base 1`, `reads` growing; note `incomplete` and `held` (question 1).
3. With T: **invite** it, wait a minute, **promote** it, change its **public note**, have it **gain a level** (twice in one day if you can), later **remove** it. Wait up to six minutes after each change (events are written after 60–300 s).
4. Open T's **member card** on O: the **Timeline** box shows joined, rank (old and new name), note (old and new text), level (one line per day) and left, newest first, with *Member since*. **This is the first half of the exit criterion.** Is the box well placed, on the three themes (Nebula, Aurora, Accord)? The tooltip names the observers, never a technical ID. Are the times UTC and right?
5. **Show all** opens the **Timeline** page of the **Officers** tab on that player; the filters by kind and period work.
6. O2 online at the same time: after the changes there is **one line per change**, with both observers in the tooltip once the data is shared. The *Officer channel* line says *working* or *not proven yet*; note which, and after how long (question 2).
7. `/reload` just after a change: the event is not lost.
8. With a big roster, play a while on O with the guild busy: no stutter (the addon reads the roster at most every 30 s).

## 2. Inactive accounts and absences (M8.3, exit criterion)

1. O: the **Officers** tab is there, with **Inactive**, **Queue** and **Timeline**; M has **no** such tab and no officer box on any card.
2. The player with one old character and one active alt **is not listed** as inactive. **This is the second half of the exit criterion.**
3. Compare three or four rows with the game's roster (*last online*): days, tier, the character last seen and the source in the tooltip. A member offline for months, without the addon: right number of days, or *unknown* (question 3)?
4. **Tiers…**: change them on O; O2 sees the same tiers after the sync. *Reset to defaults*.
5. Filters (lowest tier, **Show away**, **Show unknown**), the search box, sorting, the row menu, the tooltips: layout and clicks, on the three themes, in the guild window and in the standalone window.
6. **Absence of a member:** M declares one in **Settings** → **Absence** and with `/accord away <date>`; the message says it was sent to the officers (or, with none online, it arrives after M's next login). On O the player leaves the list and appears with **Show away**; the member card has the **Absence** box. M sees only their own declaration, never a tier.
7. **Absence set by an officer:** *Set absence…* from the row menu and from a card, then *Clear absence*; O2 receives it if the channel works.

## 3. Cleanup review and queue (M8.4)

1. **Review…**: step 1 (lowest tier, the two switches), step 2 (accounts by tier, characters, the note line, the last timeline changes), step 3. Layout of the three steps, on a long list too. Accounts away or with an officer character are not proposed.
2. Choose **Promote**, **Demote**, **Remove** and **Edit note** (public and officer) on T and other test characters. The ranks offered and the actions left out match what O may really do in the guild window (question 5). A note longer than 31 characters is refused; does the game's window allow more or less (question 4)?
3. **Queue**: the badge with the count; click an entry: the name is selected and **Ctrl+C** copies it; the command or the note text is right.
4. **Type the command** in the chat for one entry of each kind: `/gremove`, `/gpromote`, `/gdemote` work as written, also with a name with a space or from another realm (question 4).
5. **Open the guild window** from the entry: it opens, no "action blocked", now and in combat (question 6).
6. **Ticked by the roster:** after each real change the entry becomes **done** (within a minute or so); the officer-note one ticks itself (question 7), and with a rank that cannot read officer notes the entry has **Mark done**. A change made by O2 ticks O's entry too. A character that left with a rank change waiting shows **left**.
7. **Cancel entry** works. (Expiry after 14 days and removal after 7 need no test unless you wait that long.)
8. The addon itself never changes anything in the roster.

## 4. Demotion, re-promotion and members (M8.2 to M8.5)

Do this after step 5, so that there is a note to lose.

1. With data on O2 (timeline, an absence, tiers, a queue entry, a note), **demote** O2 below officer (all their characters in the guild): after the roster update one chat line says the officer data was removed; the tab, the boxes and an open export box are gone; `/accord debug` → *Officer tools* shows no records; no Lua error.
2. **Promote** O2 again: with the channel working, the history, the tiers, the absences and the notes **come back** from O (note how long; the queue does not come back).
3. On **M** at any time: `/accord debug` → *Officer tools* has no timeline, notes or tiers records; no officer message appears.

## 5. Extended officer notes (M8.5)

1. On a member card, **Write a note…**: type **several lines** (Enter), accented letters, up to the limit; the count follows; the **mouse wheel** scrolls; the box stays inside the window (question 8). Over 1000 bytes it is refused with its message.
2. Save, reopen, **Edit the note…**, **Delete the note**. The author and age are shown; the notes of the player's other characters appear read-only below.
3. The **book icon** is drawn on the Inactive page (not a code or an empty square) with its tooltip line; step 2 of the review shows the note on one line, with room above the timeline lines.
4. **Between officers:** O writes, O2 receives it on the **officer channel**; O2 edits, O sees the later version. If the channel is *not proven*, the note stays on O and the box says so. **M never receives or sees it** (question 2).
5. The note of T, removed from the guild, stays and says when it left.

## 6. Export (M8.6)

1. The **Export** button on **Roster**, **Inactive** and **Timeline**: it is where it should be (the toolbar and filter lines wrap well), M does not have it.
2. The box opens with the text **selected**; the format selector switches **CSV** and **Discord**; Esc closes it.
3. **CSV:** copy a long export (the whole roster) into a file and into a spreadsheet: complete, accents right, quotes and commas kept, a note starting with `=` shown as text. How long was the longest text, did the box cut it, did the game slow down (question 9)? A very large one says how many rows are left out.
4. A public note containing **`|`** is copied back with **one** `|` (question 9).
5. **Discord:** paste one part (1900 characters at most) into a channel: one code block, columns aligned, accepted in one message.
6. Search the pasted texts: no officer note, no account code, no technical ID.
7. The backup box of M7 (*Settings* → *Backup*) still exports and imports ([M7](M7.md) step 4): it shares the same text field.

## 7. Bank requests from the bank, with a tip (M8.7)

1. On M, a **Withdraw**: a click on the item box opens the **list**, typing filters it, the mouse wheel scrolls it, a click elsewhere or Esc closes it; it is drawn above the guild window and the standalone window, and typing still works while it is open.
2. The quantities match the vault (question 10). The quantity is **capped** while typing and when an item is picked; the line under the box shows the stock and the age, with its tooltip. A pasted link and an item number still work.
3. Send requests with and without a **tip** (a wrong tip is refused). A member without officer rank sees **no** tip of the others.
4. On O, the **Inbox**: date and time (your local time), requester, item, quantity, tip; **Oldest first**, **Newest first** and **Highest tip** sort it. The two-line rows are readable in the guild window and in the smallest standalone window; a cut text has its tooltip.
5. M **deposits the tip** at the vault; after the next capture O sees **Looks paid** (question 10; note the name and the time the money log gave). One deposit pays one tip. Nothing moves and nothing is recorded by itself.
6. If you have an **older version** on one client: it shows the request without the tip, and after that officer approves it the tip is still there for the others.
7. `/accord demo`: the Inbox shows two requests with a tip.

## 8. Taint, combat, saved data and the guild's size

1. Use the Officers tab, the dialogs, the export box and the item list in and out of combat: no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` on O and M: everything comes back; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`.
3. Note the saved file size and the *Officer tools* and *Timeline* lines after a week or two of use (the timeline is kept forever: [SPEC-10 R12](../specs/SPEC-10-risks-open-questions.md)).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or "action blocked" | the error text and what you did |
| A change missing from the timeline, or wrong | what changed and when; `/accord debug` → *Timeline* |
| A wrong number of inactive days | what the game's roster says and what the row and its tooltip say |
| Data not reaching the other officer, or reaching a member | `/accord debug` → *Officer tools* and *Sync* on both clients |
| A queue entry not ticked | the entry, the change you made, whether your rank reads officer notes |
| A command or permission wrong | what you typed and the game's answer |
| A box, list or dialog badly laid out | a screenshot, the theme and the window |
| A cut or wrong export | the format, the length and where you pasted it |
| A wrong bank quantity or no *Looks paid* | the vault, the form, the money log line |
