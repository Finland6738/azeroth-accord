# M4 in-game check: likes, the social index, the group map and suggested teammates

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M4** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.4.0` is tagged for the owner's guild ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)); until then there is no release. (`v0.1.0`, `v0.2.0` and `v0.3.0` wait for [the M1 check](M1.md), [the M2 check](M2.md) and [the M3 check](M3.md) in the same way: run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M4:** in game, two members like a third through an online officer and every client shows the same total; the social index shows for members with enough data.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the M4.7 box in [M4-reputation.md](../roadmap/M4-reputation.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)).
- **At least three clients with the addon in the same guild**, at the same time for most steps:
  - **O**, an **officer** (or the Guild Master): the notary who records likes. A **second officer O2** is needed for step 2 (the secret between officers) and is useful for step 4;
  - **A** and **B**, two members (not officers) who give likes;
  - **C**, the member who receives them (C may be one of the testers' alts on another account, or a guildmate **without** the addon: step 3.7).
  A second account of one tester is fine for any role. If the clients are guildmates, a private message with their `/accord debug` text is enough: it contains no names.
- For step 5 (the social index): group play with guildmates during the days before the check. The addon has been recording it since M2; the index needs **5 hours** with guildmates in the last 30 days, counted per guildmate (an hour in a party with four guildmates counts four).
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder (as for the M1 check), then from the repository folder:

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

  Run the second command again after every update. `/accord debug` never contains player, guild, BattleTag or Discord names, so its text is safe to paste.

## 1. Loading and `/accord debug`

1. Start the game (or `/reload`) on every client. **No Lua error**, no chat message at login.
2. `/accord debug`, Ctrl+C, paste into the report. Check these lines on each client:
   - **Features**: `officerAddonChannel` (`unknown` or `available`);
   - **APIs**: `group.InviteUnit`, `chat.ChatFrame_OpenChat`: `available` or `missing`?
   - **Notary**, **Likes**, **Social**, **GroupMap**: present, no errors;
   - **Saved data**: `no problems`.

## 2. The officers' secret (LIKE-06, LIKE-07, S5)

1. Log in **O** (and later **O2**). Wait one minute. On O: `/accord debug` → *Notary*: "officer, 1 salt(s) held". If nobody else held one, "created 1".
2. Log in **O2** (another officer). Within a minute O2 holds **the same** number of salts and "created 0" (it received it). If `officerAddonChannel` stayed `unknown`, the line says *(salt also by whisper)* and the salt came by whisper: write that down.
3. On a **member** client (A): *Notary* says "not an officer, 0 salt(s) held". It must stay 0 for the whole check.
4. If you can: demote O2 to a member rank and back. While a member, O2's *Notary* says 0 salts; promoted again, it asks and gets the salt back within a minute.

## 3. Likes (LIKE-01..LIKE-05, LIKE-08, LIKE-09)

1. On **A**, log out every officer first (or ask them to wait). Open **C**'s card (Roster → click C): the **Likes** section shows "Likes received: 0" and **[Like this player]**. Click it: it turns into *Click again to confirm*; click again. The chat says the like is on its way; the card says **waiting for an officer**. The Roster's Likes column for C is **grey**, and in `/accord reputation` C's row says **pending**.
2. `/reload` on A: still pending (the queue is saved).
3. Log **O** in. Within about a minute (A must hear O's addon; then the next delivery pass, every 15 s): A's card for C says **You liked this player.**, the Likes column is **green**, the total is **1**. On O, `/accord debug` → *Likes*: "as notary: heard 1, recorded 1".
4. On **B**, give C a like from the **Reputation** view (`/accord reputation`, click **Like** twice on C's row). Within seconds the total is **2** on B.
5. **Every client shows the same total for C (2)**: A, B, C itself, O, O2. Check C's row in the Reputation view and the Likes column on each. **This is the exit criterion.**
6. While B's like waits (repeat 4 with another target D while O is logged out), log O out right after B clicks, and in again: the like is delivered later; tell us if the chat printed "No player named … is currently playing" on B.
7. A guildmate **without the addon** can receive likes (LIKE-09): like them from A; the total shows on their row. They have no Like button (they cannot give likes).
8. Try what must not work:
   - like C again from A, or from **an alt of A** on the same account (log the alt in): there is **no Like button** on any character of C (the button is gone on every character of C's account);
   - your own characters: the Reputation row says **you**, the card has no Like action;
   - if C has an alt with the addon, log it in: the alt shows **the same total** as C (2), in the column, the card and the Reputation view.
9. `/accord debug` on every client: *Store* `likes` has the same number of entries (2 or more), *Sync* `refused 0` (a refused ledger entry means something sent a ledger that was not from an officer).

## 4. Alts linked later (the merge)

1. If a tester has a second account character that **already received a like before** its owner logged it in with the addon (or an alt that was not linked yet): log it in with the addon so the two characters list each other.
2. The totals of the two merge: a voter who liked both counts **once**. The total is the same on both characters.

## 5. The social index (SOC-04, SOC-05, SOC-06, SPEC-10 Q3)

1. On a member with **5 hours or more** with guildmates in the last 30 days: open your **own** card: the **Social index** section shows "Social index: N / 100", the effective partners "of M active players", the different partners, the time with guildmates, and the five you played with most with their share. Hover the index line: the explanation tooltip. Does it look plausible?
2. On a member with less: "not enough data (x h of the 5 h needed)".
3. `/accord reputation` → **Social**: your own index and the line "Each member sees their own index; officers see everyone's."
4. On a **member**, open someone else's card: **no** Social section, and the Roster has **no** Social column.
5. On **O** (officer): every card has the Social section; the Roster has the **Social** column (a dash under 5 hours); `/accord reputation` → **Social** lists every player by index. Do the numbers look right for players you know?
6. `/accord debug` → *Social*: "computed … in N step(s), … ms". Did the game stutter at any moment around login + 90 s?
7. Play 10 minutes in a party with a guildmate and check the minutes grow (the index updates at most every 10 minutes).

## 6. The group map (SOC-10..SOC-12, officers only)

1. On **O**: `/accord reputation` → **Groups**. The first line says how mixed the guild is (*well mixed*, *some groups*, *cliques*) with the modularity; then players, groups and hours.
2. The drawing: one circle of coloured dots per group; hover a dot for the name, click it for the card.
3. The list of groups: their players and the hours inside / with the rest of the guild. **Does it match what you know of your guild?** Write down whether the reading (*some groups* etc.) feels right.
4. On a **member**: there is **no Groups** button.

## 7. Play with someone new (SOC-20)

1. `/accord reputation` → **Someone new**: online guildmates within 5 levels with whom you played less than 30 minutes in the last 30 days, one row per player, "never" or "N min".
2. **Whisper** opens the chat box with `/w Name ` (nothing is sent). **Invite** sends a party invitation (the guildmate sees it) and the chat says so.
3. Nobody suitable online: the message says so.

## 8. Taint and combat (short; the M1 list is the full one)

After using the views in the guild window's Accord tab: do two protected actions in **Blizzard's own** guild window (edit a note; promote or invite as an officer). Nothing should say "action blocked" or raise a Lua error. Give a like and press Invite during combat: no Lua error (the invite may be refused by the game in combat: tell us what happens).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error | the error text (error frame or BugSack) and what you did |
| A like stays *pending* with an officer online | `/accord debug` on the voter and on the officer: *Notary*, *Likes*, *Sync*, Features `officerAddonChannel` |
| Two clients show different totals | `/accord debug` *Store* and *Sync* lines of both, and who was online |
| The officers hold different salts, or one says `created` twice | the *Notary* lines of both officers |
| The like was refused with a message in the chat | the message |
| The social index is missing or looks wrong | the *Social* and *CoPlay* lines, and roughly how long you played with guildmates |
| The group map looks wrong | the *GroupMap* line and a screenshot (blur names if you like) |
| Invite or Whisper does nothing | what the chat says, APIs `group.InviteUnit`, `chat.ChatFrame_OpenChat` |
| Something looks wrong on screen | a screenshot (blur names if you like) |
