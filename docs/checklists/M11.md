# M11 in-game check: guild handbook, loot page, FDCP

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M11** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.11.0` is tagged ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)). (The checks of the earlier versions, [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md), [the release check](release.md), [M7](M7.md), [M8](M8.md), [M9](M9.md) and [M10](M10.md), are open too: run those first, or all together. Where [the M10 check](M10.md) expects no Raid tab and no *Loot (FDCP)*, that is `v0.10.0`: from this version they are there.)

**Exit criterion of M11:**

- an officer edits the rules page, and a member who logs in later reads the new version marked as updated;
- the loot page names the guild's loot addon, and officers see who in the guild has it loaded.

Run steps 1 to 10 with a guild that **does not use FDCP**, and step 11 with FDCP chosen as the loot addon. One guild can do both, one after the other.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M11.8 box in [M11-loot-handbook.md](../roadmap/M11-loot-handbook.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)); the Action Center and the guild addons of M9 ([the M9 check](M9.md)); the season and the sources of M10 ([the M10 check](M10.md)).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**:
  - **O**, an officer (the Guild Master helps), and a second officer **P**;
  - **A**, a member who will become a writer by rank, and **B**, a plain member; one of them also logs a **second character of the same account**;
  - one client that **logs in only after** the pages are written (the exit criterion).
- A **rank without officer chat** that A has, and a way to rename a rank (Guild Master).
- **Finland Dragon Credit Points** on O's and B's computers, with a season and credits, for step 11.
- Some of the other loot addons, to confirm their folder names: **Gargul**, **RaidCore**, **TLC - Loot Council** from CurseForge, and any other loot addon on CurseForge for the *Other* choice.
- One client still on **`v0.10.0`** (steps 1 and 12), and a copy of the saved variables made with `v0.10.0`.
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).

`/accord demo` has **no invented data** for the handbook: nothing here can be checked in demo mode.

## 1. Upgrade from v0.10.0

1. With saved data from `v0.10.0`, install this version and `/reload`: **no Lua error**, `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`, schema **4**.
2. The settings of `v0.10.0` are as before. Roster, recipes, likes, bank, season, events, availability, notices and groups are all still there.
3. New: the **Handbook** and **Raid** tabs; `/accord help` lists `handbook` and `raid`; the member card has *Loot (FDCP)* only when FDCP is the loot addon. The season roster's source chosen in `v0.10.0` is unchanged.
4. A client still on **`v0.10.0`** in the same guild: no error there or here; the old data keeps syncing both ways; it shows nothing of M11. Keep it online with the new clients for half an hour **after** O has written pages, chosen the writers (step 6) and the loot addon (step 8), then read `/accord debug` → *Sync* and *Store* on both: **no endless repair**, no growing count of refused records, nobody ignored (question 6). This was not tried in the simulator.

## 2. Tabs and commands

1. In the **guild window** and in the standalone window (920 wide and shrunk to 720): the tabs do not run under the search box or outside the window; the ones without room are behind **More** (*Altro*); the open view's tab is always visible (question 1).
2. `/accord handbook`, `/accord handbook rules` (a page name), a name that does not exist, and `/accord raid`. Close and reopen the window: it opens on the view you left. Check English and Italian, an officer and a member, the three themes.

## 3. Default pages, once (M11.5)

1. Before anything: **B** sees *No handbook pages yet.* and no button. **O** sees *Create the default pages* only **2 minutes** after login (also after a `/reload`) (question 11).
2. **O** clicks it: six pages (Guild info, Rules, FAQ, Raid management, Roster management, Loot management) in O's language, marked *Template*, with headings only and the season links. **B** does not see them.
3. **O** and **P** try to create them at about the same time (do it in a second guild, or skip): no duplicate, no conflict notice.
4. **O** saves one template (it becomes visible to B) and **deletes** another. The button never comes back. After a `/reload`, after P logs in later and after a character that never saw the pages logs in, the deleted page **stays deleted**.

## 4. Reader, search and the "updated" mark (M11.3)

1. **B** opens each published page: headings, bullets on two levels, numbered lines, paragraphs, accented letters, in the three themes; a long page scrolls smoothly, nothing overlaps (question 2).
2. Links: to a page, to a page with a heading (it scrolls there), to the season (*Season* opens), `[[loot]]`, to a page that does not exist or is a template (grey, not clickable), a web address (copy box; no browser opens). **Back** returns.
3. Search with 1 character, with 2, with and without accents and capitals; a result opens the page at that line. The loot system's and addon's names are found (after step 8).
4. A new member sees every page **Updated**, with the count on the tab and a row in the Action Center. A page left open for 2 seconds loses the mark; clicking quickly through the list does not. **Mark all as read** clears them. O's own edits are not marked for O, nor for O's second character.
5. With **every other client logged out**, B reads every page received before.

## 5. Editor, preview, limits, revisions (M11.4)

1. Only writers see **New page**, **Edit**, **Revisions**, **Delete**, the arrows and *Deleted pages*. The editor does not open in combat.
2. Write a page with every kind of line; type `[[page|text]]` with the `|` key. **Preview** matches what B then reads. The saved link shows its text.
3. Paste a long text (more than 4000 bytes): nothing is cut, the count turns red, **Save** is off with the reason. Fill a page to exactly the limit with accented letters and save it: it arrives whole (questions 3 and 5). Try a title over 40 bytes, an empty title, a 21st page, and more than 32 000 bytes in all.
4. Paste an **item link** and a coloured text: saved as `[Name]` and plain text; no `|` anywhere (question 4).
5. Type without saving, `/reload` (or close the client): **Restore draft** brings it back. **Cancel** asks once more when the text changed.
6. **Revisions**: the ones this computer saw (at most 5 past ones); **Restore** writes a **new** revision that everybody receives. **Delete** asks to confirm; the page leaves every list; **Restore** from *Deleted pages* brings it back.
7. The arrows move a page for everybody.

## 6. Writers by rank (M11.2)

1. By default only officers and the Guild Master write. A member has no **Writers** button.
2. **O** opens **Writers** and adds A's rank (officer ranks cannot be removed). Within seconds **A** gets the editor and B does not. A's edit reaches everybody.
3. Remove the rank: A loses the editor at once (an open draft is kept), A's earlier pages stay.
4. Choose it again, then the Guild Master **renames** the rank: A cannot write; officers read *A chosen rank changed*. Choosing it again fixes it.
5. The Writers menu and the rank names are readable in both languages.

## 7. Conflicts and sync (exit criterion)

1. **Exit:** with the last client offline, **O** edits the **Rules** page. The member logs in later: within a few minutes the page is there, **marked updated**, with O's name and date; opening it clears the mark.
2. **O** and **P** open the editor on the same page. O saves; then P saves: P is told the page changed, sees O's text, and chooses **Keep mine** (it replaces O's as a new revision) or **Discard mine** (kept in P's revisions, marked *lost*).
3. A true conflict: both save within a second or two (or both while one is not receiving, for example during a boss fight). Every client ends with the **same** text; only the loser gets the notice and the Action Center row, which opens their own text in the revisions; **Restore** or **Dismiss** clears it.
4. A page written during a boss fight leaves only after it ends.
5. A member who logs in for the first time receives the whole handbook (allow 10 minutes before their client asks, plus the sending). Time it, with a large handbook.

## 8. The loot page (M11.6, exit criterion)

1. With nothing chosen: B sees no line about addons (and no *Loot system* line); a writer sees *Not set* and the **FDCP suggestion** with its address; officers also **Choose FDCP** and **Choose a loot addon**. A writer who is not an officer has no button. The suggestion is in each client's own language and is not in the page's text.
2. **O** changes the **loot system** (each value; *Other* with a name) and **Save**. Every client shows it.
3. **O** chooses a known addon (for example Gargul). **Exit:** the page names it; **O** sees **who has it** loaded with versions, **who lacks it**, and how many are not known; **B** sees the counts, versions and their own line only. With **Share my addon list** off, or the guild list switched off, one line says so. The numbers match what the testers really have.
4. The **download** box: there for writers always, for others while the addon is not loaded; select and Ctrl+C works; typing in it puts the address back.
5. **Other (from CurseForge)** with name, folder and address: refused with a clear message for an `http://` address, another site or a folder with spaces; accepted for a real CurseForge page. Its *who has it* finds the folder (also typed with other capitals); a wrong folder gets its own line.
6. The fixed part scrolls with the text, nothing overlaps, the menus open in the right place (question 9). Narrow window and both languages.
7. **Delete** the Loot management page (or look as B while it is still a template): **Raid** → **Loot page** shows the same fixed part, and **Back** returns.

## 9. The loot addons' folders and addresses

1. For each loot addon you can install, **write down the real folder name** and version, and compare with `Data/Integrations.lua`; look at **Raid** → *Raid and loot addons in the guild* (question 7).
2. Open each download address in a browser (question 8).
3. Nothing of any other addon changes: no setting, no saved data, no message.

## 10. The Raid view (M11.7)

1. At the top the *Season roster* source (officers change it, members see it locked), the loot addon's name and **Loot page**. There is no *Loot* source selector any more.
2. With the season roster from Accord, the view points to **Season**.

## 11. With FDCP (M11.7)

1. FDCP loaded on O's and B's clients. **O** presses **Choose FDCP** (or chooses it with *Change*): every client follows, on the loot page, the Raid view and the member card.
2. **O**: the loot page shows *Loaded* with the version, **Open FDCP**, O's credits and place, and the **rules summary** marked as the guild's; compare every line with FDCP's window. **B**: the same with the Patto preset's defaults and the line that says so (question 10). Change a rule or award loot in FDCP: Accord follows without a `/reload`. The rules never appear in the page's text or in the editor.
3. Member card: **Loot (FDCP)** with the credits and place FDCP shows; an alt shows its main's; an inactive main has no place; a member FDCP does not know gets a line saying so.
4. **Raid** view with FDCP as the season roster's source too: season, pool (MS1, MS2, OS on O's client, MS1 only on B's), lineup and bench of the open night on O's client.
5. **Missing:** B removes FDCP: *Not installed*, the download box, no credits, no error. **Switched off:** *Not loaded* and a line that says why, no error.
6. **Unknown API version** (only if you can: edit `API_VERSION` in a copy of FDCP to 2 on a test client): level 0, a line asks to update Accord, the rest works; restore FDCP afterwards.
7. **Load order:** `/reload` several times, log out and in, switch the two addons off and on one at a time: FDCP's data is there each time, with no error. If your client lets you force Accord to load before FDCP, try it.
8. **O** chooses another loot addon or none: *Loot (FDCP)*, the credits and the rules leave every client; FDCP's own data is unchanged.

## 12. Taint, saved data, traffic and size

1. Use every new view, list, box, menu and button in and out of combat, then the guild window, the game and FDCP: no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` and a full logout: pages, read marks, revisions and the draft come back; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`.
3. *Clear guild data*: the handbook comes back from the others, no deleted page returns, the default pages are not offered while pages exist.
4. A backup export and import (M7) does not carry local revisions or drafts and gives no error.
5. In a large guild, note the saved file size and, in `/accord debug` → *Sync* and *Sending*, the traffic the evening the version is installed, while the handbook is first written, and some days later (questions 5 and 6).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or "action blocked" | the error text and what you did |
| A tab, page, form, menu or box badly laid out | a screenshot, the theme, the language and the window |
| A page shows wrong formatting or a dead link | the page's text (copied from the editor) and a screenshot |
| A page missing, old or duplicated | page name, revision and author on both clients; `/accord debug` → *Sync* and *Store* on both |
| Conflict not told, or text lost | who saved what and when; both clients' revisions |
| A writer refused, or a non-writer accepted | the ranks, the chosen writers; `/accord debug` → *Store* |
| Loot addon not detected or *who has it* wrong | its real folder name and version; `/accord debug` → *Integrations* |
| FDCP data wrong | FDCP's version and `/accord debug` → *Integrations*, *FDCP* |
| Upgrade problem | `/accord debug` → *Saved data* and the old saved file |
