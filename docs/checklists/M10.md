# M10 in-game check: season roster, game calendar, Raid-Helper, bench, availability, rallies, raid and loot addons

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M10** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.10.0` is tagged ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)). (The checks of the earlier versions, [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md), [the release check](release.md), [M7](M7.md), [M8](M8.md) and [M9](M9.md), are open too: run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M10:**

- three members declare MS1, MS2 and OS for the season, and an officer sees the role counts and the gaps;
- a member signs up to an event as tentative from Accord, and the same answer shows in the game's calendar;
- an officer pastes a Raid-Helper event; the sign-ups are matched to members by their Discord name and copied into the game's calendar;
- with Finland Dragon Credit Points (FDCP) loaded and chosen as the source, the season roster shows FDCP's declarations and Accord asks for none.

Run it **twice**: with a guild that **does not use FDCP** (steps 1 to 10) and with a guild that **does** (step 11). One guild can do both: first with FDCP switched off on every client and *Season roster* set to *Accord*, then with FDCP on and chosen.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M10.10 box in [M10-calendar-raids.md](../roadmap/M10-calendar-raids.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)); the contacts with Discord names ([M2](M2.md)); the Action Center and the guild addons of M9 ([the M9 check](M9.md)).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**:
  - **O**, an officer (a Guild Master helps), and if possible a second officer **P**;
  - **A**, **B** and **C**, three members of different classes; one of them also logs a **second character of the same account** (step 4).
- One member **without the addon**, who answers in the game's calendar (step 5).
- **Finland Dragon Credit Points** on O's and A's computers, for step 11.
- A real **Raid-Helper** event on the guild's Discord with a few sign-ups of several states (signed up, late, bench, tentative, absence), and access to its JSON and CSV export (step 6). **Keep a copy of the export**: it settles the format. Do not attach it to a public issue as it is: replace names and ids first.
- One client still on **`v0.9.0`** (step 1), and a copy of the saved variables made with `v0.9.0`.
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).

`/accord demo` has **no invented data** for the M10 pages: nothing here can be checked in demo mode.

## 1. Upgrade from v0.9.0

1. With saved data from `v0.9.0`, install this version and `/reload`: **no Lua error**, `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`, schema **4**.
2. The settings of `v0.9.0` are as before; **Share when I usually play** is new and **on**. Roster, recipes, likes, bank, timeline, officer notes, notices and groups are all still there.
3. `/accord help` lists `season`, `events` and `availability`, and **not** `raid`. There is **no Raid tab** and no *Loot (FDCP)* on the member card, even with FDCP installed. `/accord debug` has the *Integrations* and *FDCP* sections.
4. A client still on **`v0.9.0`** in the same guild: no error there or here; the old data keeps syncing both ways; it shows nothing of M10. Let it stay online with the new clients for half an hour after they have written season, event and availability data, then read `/accord debug` → *Sync* on both: **no endless repair**; note the bytes per minute (question 11: older clients refuse the new data, so this is not proven).

## 2. Tabs and commands

1. In the **guild window** and in the standalone window (920 wide and shrunk to 720): the tabs do not run under the search box or outside the window; the ones without room are behind **More** (*Altro*); the open view's tab is always visible (question 1).
2. Every new view opens from its tab or the menu and from its command: `/accord season`, `/accord events`, `/accord availability`. Check English and Italian, an officer and a member, the three themes.

## 3. Raid and loot addons (M10.2)

1. With some of the known addons installed (Gargul, RaidCore, WLC, Forever Loot Manager, NostalgiaForeverLC, RollForMe, FDCP): at the bottom of **Season**, *Raid and loot addons in the guild* shows each one's state on this client (loaded, switched off, installed, not installed), the version and the level. For each installed one, **write down the real folder name** and compare it with `Data/Integrations.lua` (question 2).
2. Switch one off, `/reload`: *switched off*. Remove it: *not installed*.
3. After one or two minutes the counts show how many members who share their addon list have each one, with the versions. **O** sees who has it, by name; **A** sees the counts and their own entry only. With **Share my addon list** off the line says so.
4. Nothing of any other addon changes: no setting, no saved data, no message.

## 4. Season roster (M10.3, M10.4, exit criterion)

1. A member sees *No season is open*. **O** writes a name and presses **Open season**; the source is *Accord*. A member cannot change the source or open or close a season.
2. **A**, **B** and **C** declare MS1 with its role, MS2, OS and a note (**Declare**, then **Confirm**); one uses **Not sure yet** first and declares after. The tree names and the roles offered are right for each class; check as many classes as possible (question 3). The popups of the lists open correctly.
3. Everybody sees the three declarations within a few seconds. **Nobody can change another player's declaration.** A second character of the same account cannot declare.
4. Within 30 minutes A corrects MS1; after 30 minutes the specs are locked with the date, and **Save note** still works.
5. **O** sets a **target composition** (10, 20, 25 or 40 and the four roles; **Set target**): the counts by MS1, *MS2 would add*, the gaps and *MS2 covers* match the declarations by hand; *Not sure yet* apart; the members without a declaration in their own list, one name per account, **never counted as a gap**. A member sees the same counts but no target editor and no **Export**.
6. **Export**: CSV and Discord text; copy with Ctrl+C, paste into a spreadsheet and into Discord, with accented names.
7. **Open season** again: the new one is empty, the previous one stays readable in the picker, without counts. **Close season** closes the current one.
8. A member who logs in later receives the season and the declarations. *What I share* lists your declaration.

## 5. Events on the game calendar (M10.5, exit criterion)

1. **O** creates a guild sign-up event in the **game's** calendar for the next days; another member creates an ordinary guild event. Within a minute of login, **Events** lists both, from 7 days ago to 14 ahead, with your answer. Do it once **without ever opening** the game's calendar window (question 4). No error and no "action blocked".
2. **Exit:** **A** presses **Tentative** in Accord: *Saved*, and **the game's calendar shows Tentative**. Then **Sign up** and **Decline**, on a sign-up event and on an invitation; also "Sign up" after another answer (question 6). If the game's calendar opens instead, write down on which button.
3. **B** answers in the game's window; the member **without the addon** too: Accord shows the same answers. For every state the game offers (invited, accepted, declined, confirmed, standby, signed up, not signed up, tentative, out) compare what Accord shows (question 5).
4. The role is preselected from MS1; from MS2 when MS1's role is covered by the target and MS2's is not; from the class for a member without a declaration, who **can sign up**. A role you change stays chosen; the others see it, with the roles signed up.
5. Keep the game's calendar window open, then try in combat: Accord waits and says why. Open an event in the game while Accord reads it: no stuck or wrong event. **Open game calendar** works.
6. If the events **never** load: after a minute O can create one in Accord, marked **Not in the game calendar**; others answer it. This path should not appear on a client with a working calendar: if it does, report it.

## 6. Raid-Helper import (M10.6, exit criterion)

1. **O**: **Events** → **Import from Raid-Helper**. Open `https://raid-helper.xyz/api/v4/events/<event id>`, copy everything, paste with Ctrl+V, **Check**. The whole text arrives, also for a large event (question 8). Title, date, **time in the realm's time**, matched count and unmatched names are right (question 7).
2. Compare every sign-up with Discord: signed up, late, bench, tentative, absence; class, spec and role. Accented names and emoji.
3. Do the same with the **CSV** export (write the title and the date in the box). Note its header line.
4. Matching: by the Discord name of the contacts, by character name, one entry per account on the right character. Match one name by hand (**Match**); it is remembered at the next import.
5. **Import**: **A** sees their own Discord state; O also sees the lists, the roles against season and target, and the three differences with the game calendar once the event is chosen. **A** and O's second computer or **P** receive the event; the unmatched names are **not** on P's client.
6. Paste something else, a cut export and an empty box: a clear message, nothing saved, no error.

## 7. Into the game calendar (M10.6, exit criterion)

1. With **no** game event at that date and time: **Put into the game calendar**, read the sentence, **Confirm**. Is the event created, with title and time right, the members invited and their states written (question 9)? Compare in the game's window, on O's and on A's client.
2. With an existing event of O's at that time: it is used, **no second event**. With one O cannot edit: a message, nothing created.
3. A member who **already answered in the game** keeps that answer, and the view shows the difference.
4. More than 40 members (if possible): 40 per confirmation. Open the calendar window or enter combat while it writes: it stops and says who was left out.
5. As **P** (another officer rank) repeat the write. If a call is refused: note the message; the members find *Answer in the game calendar* in the Action Center, and it leaves when they answer.
6. **Export** on the event: the Discord text and CSV paste well.

## 8. Bench and composition (M10.7)

1. **O** chooses an event: *Signed up, not placed* holds the yes and tentative of the game and the matched Discord sign-ups, not who declined in the game. Place players with **To raid** and **To bench**, **Remove**; the counts against the target are right; the **MS2** offers appear for a short role and *as ... (MS2)* places them so.
2. **A**, **B** and **C** see *Your place*, the raid and the bench; they have no buttons. A player who changes answer is marked *no longer signed up*.
3. **Write to calendar**, **Confirm**: members without a game answer become confirmed (raid) or standby (bench); who answered in the game is **unchanged** (question 9).
4. The export has the *Line-up* column. The officers' row (choose a player, three buttons, **Write to calendar**) and the player menu fit at the smallest window, with 30 or more names (this layout was never seen on a screen).

## 9. Availability and rallies (M10.8, M10.9)

1. **Availability**: *Mine* fills while you are online (an hour counts after 10 minutes in a week); the hour is the **realm's**. The map is published a few minutes after login, then at most once a day in the first week: look at *Guild* again the next day for the counts; **O** sees the names of a cell, **A** only the count and their own level. The grid reads well in both languages and the three themes; clicks choose the right cell.
2. **Share when I usually play** off (Settings or *What I share*): your map leaves the guild's counts; *Mine* keeps counting; on again, it returns. Log out and in: the minutes are kept (the saved file has no error).
3. Nothing anywhere mentions raids attended.
4. **Events** → **Rallies**: A calls a PvP rally with a meeting point, starting in 15 minutes. B gets the chat line and the Action Center row, answers **Coming**; C **Not coming**; everybody sees who. **Change** and **Close** for the caller only; a second rally closes the first; the fifth in an hour is refused.
5. A rally **ends by itself** and is gone 10 minutes later on every client. A member who logs in during one sees it, and the answers after opening the page. The times shown are right; no position or map anywhere.
6. The notice *A new rally is called* can be switched off in Settings; in combat and quiet hours it waits.

## 10. Boss fights

1. During a boss encounter let another client write a declaration, and on the fighting client try an answer and a rally: nothing leaves until the fight ends, then it arrives (question 10). The Events view says *Not during combat or a boss*.
2. FDCP and the raid addons work as usual during the fight; no chat or addon throttling.

## 11. With FDCP as the source (exit criterion)

1. FDCP loaded on O's and A's clients, with a season and a pool. **O** changes *Season roster* to **FDCP** in **Season**: every client follows. A member cannot change it. Only *Season roster* is offered, no *Loot* source and no Raid tab.
2. **Exit:** **Season** shows FDCP's season and pool; there is **no declaration form**. **Open FDCP** works. On O's client MS1, MS2 and OS; on A's MS1 only, with a line that says why.
3. The role counts, the gaps, *Without a declaration* and the export match FDCP's pool; inactive mains are not counted; a damage dealer is melee or ranged by spec (question 12).
4. A client **without** FDCP, and one with FDCP switched off: a line says why there is nothing, no error.
5. A signs up **from Accord** to an event FDCP links to a night: **FDCP counts the sign-up** (no +1 credit for not signing up). In **Events** the line-up part says it is FDCP's, shows the open night on O's client, and offers no bench. A confirmed or standby state in the game is read by FDCP as expected.
6. A Raid-Helper import and its calendar write still work; the confirmation warns if the day already has another raid event.
7. **Availability**: O sees FDCP's declared days under a cell, labelled as a declaration, not added to the counts.
8. Back to **Accord**: the earlier declarations and line-ups show again. FDCP's own data is unchanged.

## 12. Taint, saved data, traffic and size

1. Use every new view, list, box and button in and out of combat, and the game's calendar and FDCP afterwards: no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` and a full logout: everything comes back (the pasted export does not: it is never saved); `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`.
3. *Clear guild data*: no old season, event or rally comes back by mistake or is announced again.
4. In a large guild note the saved file size and the sync traffic in the first evening and some days later (question 11).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or "action blocked" | the error text and what you did |
| A tab, list, grid or box badly laid out | a screenshot, the theme, the language and the window |
| A detected addon missing or wrong | its real folder name and version; `/accord debug` → *Integrations* |
| A declaration or count wrong or missing | class, tree, role; `/accord debug` → *Sync* on both clients |
| Calendar events or answers wrong | the answer in Accord and in the game's window (screenshots), the kind of event |
| A Raid-Helper export refused or read wrong | the export with **names and ids replaced**, and the message |
| A write into the calendar refused | the message, your rank, the kind of event |
| Availability or rally wrong | the time shown and the realm's time; `/accord debug` → *Sync* |
| FDCP data wrong | FDCP's version and `/accord debug` → *Integrations*, *FDCP* |
| Upgrade problem | `/accord debug` → *Saved data* and the old saved file |
