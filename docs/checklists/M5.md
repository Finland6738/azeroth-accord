# M5 in-game check: the guild bank (snapshots, logs, donations, requests)

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M5** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.9.0` (the beta for the owner's guild) is tagged ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)); until then there is no release. (`v0.1.0` to `v0.4.0` wait for [the M1 check](M1.md), [the M2 check](M2.md), [the M3 check](M3.md) and [the M4 check](M4.md) in the same way: run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M5:** in game, a member who never visits the vault sees the bank contents captured by another member, creates a withdrawal request, and an officer approves and fulfils it.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the M5.7 box in [M5-guild-bank.md](../roadmap/M5-guild-bank.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)).
- A guild bank with **at least three tabs**, of which: one every rank can view, one only officers can view, and (best) one for an in-between rank; some items in each; a few recent deposits and withdrawals in the logs (do them yourself before the check: deposit 20 of a cheap item, take 5 out, deposit some gold).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**, at the same time for most steps:
  - **O**, an **officer** (or the Guild Master), who can open the vault; a **second officer O2** is needed for steps 3 and 4;
  - **A**, a **member** (not an officer) who can open the vault and view at least the open tab: the one who captures;
  - **B**, a **member who never opens the vault** during the check: the one who sees what A captured;
  - **C**, optionally a member of a rank that cannot view the officers' tab (the same as B is fine if B cannot).
  A second account of one tester is fine for any role. `/accord debug` never contains player or guild names: a private message with its text is enough.
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder (as for the M1 check), then from the repository folder:

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py
```

```bash
python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py
```

  Run the second command again after every update.

## 1. Loading and `/accord debug`

1. Start the game (or `/reload`) on every client. **No Lua error**, no chat message at login.
2. `/accord debug`, Ctrl+C, paste into the report. Check these lines:
   - **Features**: `bankTabs` and `bankLogs` (`unknown` until the vault is opened), `officerAddonChannel`;
   - **Bank**, **BankLog**, **BankRequests**, **BankInbox**: present, no errors;
   - **Saved data**: `no problems`.
3. `/accord bank`: the **Bank** view opens; with no data it says that the game shows the bank only at the vault.

## 2. Capture by a member (BANK-01)

1. On **A**, open the guild vault (with `/accord debug` open in mind: do not open it in combat). Look at each tab in the game as you normally do: nothing should look different or slower, and deposits/withdrawals must still work.
2. Wait about 20 seconds (one query per second: tabs, then the money, then the logs). `/accord debug` → *Bank*: "captured: tab 1: N item(s); tab 2: …; money: published; log …". Did the game stutter during it?
3. Close the vault. `/accord bank` on A: **Tabs** shows a button for every tab A can view, the money, and "seen just now by …" with the tab laid out like in the game (14 columns of 7 slots).
4. **Compare with the game slot by slot** for one tab: same items, same positions (the first column goes down), same counts, same icons. Write down any difference.
5. Hover an item: the tooltip is the item's. Is it right for an enchanted or suffixed item (step 4 of the table)?
6. **Items** mode: one row per item with its total and the tabs it is in; type a name, an item number and a tab name in the panel's search box.
7. Open the vault again within half an hour without changing anything: nothing is published again (`/accord debug` → *Bank* "unchanged"); change something (deposit an item), reopen: the tab is published at once.

## 3. A member who never visits the vault sees it (BANK-02, BANK-03)

1. On **B**, `/accord bank` (B has not opened the vault): within a minute or two **B sees A's tabs**: the buttons, the items, the money, "seen N min ago by A". **This is half of the exit criterion.**
2. Log **A** out; log **O2** in: O2 also gets the snapshots (relayed by an officer or by A's earlier broadcast).
3. On **O** open the vault: O's snapshot of a tab goes **only to officers**: **O2** shows it in the *Tabs* mode, **B shows nothing new** for that tab (the *Store* `bank` count on B does not grow with O's records).
4. If `officerAddonChannel` stayed `unknown` or `missing`: O's snapshots stay with O (no fallback): write it down.

## 4. Permissions (Q4)

1. The officers' tab: **B** (and **C**) have **no button** for it, even after O and O2 read it.
2. The tab visible to all ranks, read **only by an officer** (O): B does not see it until a member (A) has opened the vault. Is that acceptable for your guild? (SPEC-10 Q4.)
3. If you have an in-between rank: a tab its members can view but lower ranks cannot: do lower-rank clients see it after a mid-rank member read it? (They should not.)
4. A member **cannot** make the addon show the officers' tab: only `/accord debug` *Store* tells the counts, never the contents.

## 5. Logs, donations and the money (BANK-10..BANK-12)

1. **Log** mode on A after step 2: the entries you made (deposit of 20, withdrawal of 5, gold) with plausible ages ("3 h ago"), names, items and quantities. Compare with the game's own log: same entries.
2. **Features** `bankLogs` is `available`. Write down what the game gives for the time of an entry (the report of this step is S9).
3. Wait an hour, deposit something else, open the vault on A **again**: the *Log* shows the new entry and **every old one exactly once** (no duplicate of the first visit's entries). Open it on **O** as well (a different guildmate reads the same log): still once.
4. **Donations** mode: the deposits of gold and items per player over 30 days, 365 days and all time, by gold or by items; the characters of one player (alts with the addon) appear as **one** row; withdrawals and repairs are not counted. Visible to members and officers alike (SPEC-10 Q5).
5. **Money** mode: after a few days of visits it draws bars from the money seen (a single point shows a flat bar or the "no history" text).

## 6. Requests (BANK-20..BANK-22) and the exit criterion

1. On **B**, **Requests** mode: kind *Withdraw*, paste an item link (shift-click an item into the box, or write its number), how many, a reason, **Send request**. The row appears, status *Open*, with a **Cancel** button.
2. **Every client sees it**: A, O, O2 (within about a minute), also a member who logs in later.
3. Send a second request for gold (leave the item empty, write whole gold pieces) and a *Deposit* and a *Donation*. A sixth open request is refused ("5 open requests").
4. **Cancel** one on B: it disappears from the open list (*Show closed ones too* shows it as *Cancelled*) on every client.
5. On **O**: **Approve** B's request: B's row says *Approved* within seconds on every client; **B gets a chat line** "Your bank request … was approved by …". Then **Fulfilled** (after you handed the item over by mail or trade): *Fulfilled* everywhere. **This completes the exit criterion.**
6. Reject another with a reason typed in the reason box: B's chat line carries the reason; the row's tooltip lists the history (who and when).
7. On **A** (a member): there are **no** Approve / Reject / Fulfilled buttons, only Cancel on A's own requests.
8. An alt of B (log it in, same account) can cancel B's open request.

## 7. The officers' inbox and notices (BANK-23, BANK-24)

1. While requests are open: **O** and **O2** (not B, not A) see a **red mark with the number** on the Accord tab of the guild window, "Bank (n)" on the panel button, and the line in the compartment tooltip. Does the mark sit well on the tab?
2. A new request makes **one chat line** on O and O2: "New bank request from …". Reload (`/reload`): not repeated.
3. **Inbox** mode (O only): the waiting requests, oldest first. For an approved request, do what the requester asked in the game (B takes the item out of the vault, or deposits it): after the vault is opened by anyone with the addon, the inbox row says **Looks delivered: …**. Click **Fulfilled**.
4. The mark goes away when nothing waits.

## 8. Taint, combat and saved data (short; the M1 list is the full one)

1. Open and close the vault several times, move items, deposit and withdraw as an officer and as a member: the vault must behave exactly as without the addon, no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` on A and B: tabs, logs, requests and the counter come back from the saved data; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`.
3. `/accord debug` on every client: *Store* shows the same number of `bankreq` records, *Sync* `refused 0`.

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error | the error text (error frame or BugSack) and what you did |
| A tab is empty or missing in the view | `/accord debug` → *Bank* (the "last:" line) and *Features*; was the tab viewable for that rank? |
| B does not see A's snapshots | `/accord debug` *Store* and *Sync* lines on A and B, and who was online |
| A duplicate entry in the log | the two entries as shown, and the ages the game shows for them in its own log |
| An item has no name | what *Items* shows for it (the number) and whether it appears after a while |
| A request does not reach everyone, or two clients show different statuses | `/accord debug` *Store* and *Sync* lines of both |
| The mark on the tab looks wrong | a screenshot (blur names if you like) |
| Something looks wrong on screen | a screenshot (blur names if you like) |
