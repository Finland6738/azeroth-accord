# M9 in-game check: Action Center, notices, quiet hours, login digest, guild group finder, guild addons

The offline tests and the network simulator prove that the code agrees with our assumptions about the client; this checklist proves that the client agrees with the code. It is the **exit check of milestone M9** and it is **not done yet**: nobody has run this version on WoW Forever. When it passes, `v0.9.0` is tagged ([docs/RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md)). (The checks of the earlier versions, [M1](M1.md), [M2](M2.md), [M3](M3.md), [M4](M4.md), [M5](M5.md), [the release check](release.md), [M7](M7.md) and [M8](M8.md), are open too: run those first, or all together.)

**Exit criterion of M9:** in game, a craft request and a bank request both appear in the Action Center; nothing pops up during quiet hours or combat, and the login digest lists what changed since the last session. A leader posts a dungeon group needing a tank and a healer, two members apply with those roles, the leader accepts them and they are invited; nobody outside the guild sees the group. The Guild addons table lists the addons of the members who share them, with the right counts.

Report the results with the *In-game test report* issue form (or in the table at the end), and tick the second M9.10 box in [M9-action-center.md](../roadmap/M9-action-center.md).

## What you need

- WoW Forever, and the roster of M2 working ([the M2 check](M2.md)); the bank of M5 for the bank notices ([the M5 check](M5.md)).
- **At least four clients with the addon in the same guild**:
  - **L**, a member who leads groups;
  - **T** and **H**, two members who can play a tank and a healer; one of them, or L, has a profession whose recipes the addon has read;
  - **O**, an officer (a Guild Master helps).
- **A character outside the guild**, with the addon, who is also on L's **friends list** (steps 5 and 7).
- One client still on **`v0.8.0`** (step 1), and a copy of the saved variables made with `v0.8.0`.
- Ideally a few members with **many addons** installed (step 8).
- On the PC: `tools/local.ini` with your AddOns folder, then `python -I -X utf8 tools/fetch_libs.py` and `python -I -X utf8 tools/install_addon.py` (again after every update).

`/accord demo` fills the new pages with invented data: use it to look at layouts when the real guild has nothing to show yet. Nothing is sent or saved, so it proves nothing about the real client.

## 1. Upgrade from v0.8.0

1. With saved data from `v0.8.0`, install this version and `/reload`: **no Lua error**, `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`, schema **4**.
2. The settings are as before: theme, columns, and the two bank notice switches (new request, decision) keep their values. **No old bank request is announced again.**
3. Roster, recipes, likes, bank, timeline and officer notes are all still there.
4. A client still on **`v0.8.0`** in the same guild: no error there or here; the old data keeps syncing both ways; it shows nothing of groups or addons; `/accord debug` → *Sync* on both shows no endless repair (note the bytes per minute: question 12).
5. Updated clients: `/accord help` lists `inbox`, `groups` and `addons`, and **not** `raid`. There is **no Raid tab**, no *Loot (FDCP)* on the member card and no section about integrations in `/accord debug`, even with Finland Dragon Credit Points installed.

## 2. Tabs and commands

1. In the **guild window** and in the standalone window (920 wide and shrunk to 720): the tabs do not run under the search box or outside the window; the ones without room are behind **More** (*Altro*), which opens a menu above the window, shows the sum of the hidden counts and closes on a click elsewhere or Esc (question 1).
2. Every view opens from its tab or from the menu, and from its command: `/accord inbox`, `/accord groups`, `/accord addons`. The open view's tab is always visible with its line under it. Check English and Italian, an officer and a member, the three themes.
3. The tooltip of the addon in the AddOn Compartment says *Action Center: N to do* when something waits.

## 3. Action Center and notices (M9.2, M9.3, exit criterion)

1. T presses **Request craft** on a recipe (send the whisper or not): on T's Inbox a *You asked … to craft …* reminder; **Done** removes it.
2. T sends a **bank request**: T sees *Waiting for an officer*, O sees *Bank request*. **Both a craft and a bank request show in the Action Center** (T's list has both). A click opens the right view on the right entry; after O decides, the line goes away.
3. Officer rows: a queued promotion or note ([M8](M8.md) step 3) and an ending absence appear for O only, and open the Queue, the Inactive page or the Absence card.
4. Order, colours, chips, the number on the tab, the empty text.
5. **Notices** page: the history, unread count, *All* / *Unread*, **Mark all read**, **Clear** with its second click; a click opens the view.
6. Settings → **Notifications**: chat line, on-screen box and sound per module. The box shows for 6 s at the top of the screen, a click opens, a right-click closes; the sounds play (question 2). A switched-off notice is kept as *muted*.
7. Floods: log O in with several requests waiting: one grouped notice, not one per request; never a flood.

## 4. Quiet hours, combat and instances (M9.4, exit criterion)

1. **Combat:** while O fights, send a request: nothing pops up; 3 s after combat it comes out **once**. Several notices come out in order, with three chat lines at most, one box and one sound.
2. **Instance:** the same inside a dungeon, and in a battleground or arena if possible; it comes out 5 s after leaving. With the instance switch off it shows at once outside combat (question 3).
3. **Quiet hours:** set *From* / *To* around the current time, then a range **across midnight** (for example 23 to 8, checked before and after midnight, or by moving the range around now): held inside, released within a minute of the end. A notice read meanwhile does not come out.
4. The **Notices** page has every notice at once, also while it waits. `/reload` with notices held: they stay in the history only, no error.
5. The *From* / *To* lists open correctly inside the Settings scroll area (not cut).

## 5. Login digest (M9.5, exit criterion)

1. Log O out. Meanwhile: a member joins and one leaves, T sends a request, somebody likes O, something moves in the bank. Log O in.
2. About 10 s after the roster, one chat line with numbers, and the card **Since your last session** on the Inbox: right counts, names for joined and left, **likes as a number only, never a name**; each line opens its view; **Close** removes it.
3. What guildmates bring in the first two and a half minutes completes the card (question 4). Compare the bank movement count with the game's log.
4. Log in again with nothing changed: **no line, no card, no sound**. `/reload`: no digest.
5. Log in during quiet hours or in an instance: the chat line waits. With *Summary at login* off, no chat line.

## 6. Guild group finder (M9.6, M9.7, exit criterion)

1. L: **Groups** → **Post a group**: a dungeon needing a tank and a healer. The instances offered, their levels and size are right for Forever (question 8); a **Help** group has the zone filled in (question 7).
2. T, H and O see it within a few seconds; filters, chips, search, the tooltip, the preselected role (question 7). The **character outside the guild sees nothing**.
3. T applies as tank, H as healer. L gets the notice, the *Now* line on the Inbox, the count on the tab; the applicants show class, level, role and comment.
4. L presses **Accept** on both: **the invitation arrives** (question 5), the roles needed go down, the group closes as *Full* when nobody is needed; T and H get the notice. **Decline** and **Withdraw** work.
5. With a party of 5 and a bigger size: *Convert your party to a raid first*. A non-leader cannot invite.
6. **Inside an instance:** L posts, receives an application and accepts while inside; note what arrives when (questions 5 and 6).
7. **Closing:** *Close group*; time limit (30 minutes); L logs out → *Leader offline* on the others; `/reload` of L does **not** close it. A member who logs in later sees the open groups after opening the tab.
8. *New groups that fit my filters* is off by default; switched on, it notifies at most every 5 minutes, quiet in combat.
9. Layout of the three pages in the guild window and in the standalone window, long names and notes, accented letters.

## 7. The game's Group Finder, privately (M9.8)

1. L, alone or as party leader, with a dungeon group open on **My group**: is there a button **Also list in the game's finder** (240 pt, beside *Close group*)? `/accord debug` → *Game finder* and the capability `gameFinderPrivate` (question 9).
2. If present: **one click** lists it. No "action blocked", no Lua error. Is it marked private? The **friend outside the guild** and a guild member see it; a stranger who is neither does **not**.
3. *Close group* removes it in the same click. Let a group close by itself: a notice asks to remove it, **Remove from the game's finder** works.
4. Log out with a game listing: is it still there?
5. If the game shows it as **public**: remove it, report it at once, check that the button is not offered again.
6. If there is no button: nothing about the game's finder shows anywhere. Write the result in SPEC-13 §7.3.

## 8. Guild addons (M9.9, exit criterion)

1. `/accord addons` one to two minutes after login on each client: the line says out of how many members the counts are.
2. For two or three clients compare the list with the game's AddOns list: only **loaded** ones, **no Blizzard addon**, titles without colour codes or icons, versions readable, Accord included (question 10).
3. The counts, shares and versions are right; two characters of one account count once; sorting by every column, search, tooltips.
4. A click on a row: O sees **who** uses it; a member sees only their own entry.
5. **Share my addon list** off (Settings → Contacts, or *What I share*): that member leaves the counts on the others; nothing more is sent; on again, the list returns at the next check.
6. A client with many addons: is the list cut, at how many entries (question 11)? A `v0.8.0` client sees no difference.
7. Layout of the table, the detail panel and the switch.

## 9. Taint, combat, saved data and size

1. Use every new view, menu, dialog and the toast in and out of combat: no "action blocked", no Lua error.
2. `/reload` and a full logout: everything comes back; `/accord debug` → *Saved data* `no problems`; no error in the *Actions*, *Notify*, *Digest*, *Groups* and *Census* sections.
3. *Clear guild data*: the notice history is empty, nothing old is announced again.
4. Note the saved file size and the sync traffic in a large guild (question 12).

## What to report if something does not work

| Symptom | What to send |
|---|---|
| Lua error or "action blocked" | the error text and what you did |
| A tab, menu, box or table badly laid out | a screenshot, the theme, the language and the window |
| A line missing from the Action Center or the digest | what happened and when; `/accord debug` → *Actions*, *Digest* |
| A notice at the wrong time, repeated or lost | combat, instance or hours settings; `/accord debug` → *Notify* |
| A group not seen, or no invitation | `/accord debug` → *Groups* and *Sync* on both clients, where you were |
| The game's finder button or listing wrong | who saw the listing; `/accord debug` → *Game finder* |
| A wrong addon list or count | the game's AddOns list and the row; `/accord debug` → *Census* |
| Upgrade problem | `/accord debug` → *Saved data* and the old saved file |
