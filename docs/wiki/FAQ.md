# FAQ

*Italiano: [[Domande frequenti|FAQ-it]]*

**Do all guildmates need the addon?** No. Some data comes from the game for everyone (when the game provides it). Alts, contacts, shared recipes and bank snapshots exist only for members who use the addon.

**Can I hide my alts?** No: the list of your characters is always shared with the guild (owner's decision, see Privacy and data). It only shows characters of your own Battle.net account on the realm where you played with the addon.

**Why is my alt not under my main?** The alt must have logged in once with the addon, and both characters must list each other. A guild note `main: <name>` works as a hint for alts without the addon.

**Can I remove a like?** No. Likes are permanent by design.

**Who can see that I liked someone?** Nobody in the guild list: everyone sees only totals. The one officer whose addon records your like sees that it came from you at that moment.

**My like says "pending".** No officer with the addon is online yet. It is delivered by itself when one is, also after you log out and back in.

**Does the social index say who is a good player?** No. It only measures how widely someone groups with different guildmates.

**Who can see my social index?** You, and the officers and the Guild Master. Other members see only their own.

**Does "Someone new" invite people by itself?** No. It only lists guildmates; *Whisper* opens the chat box for you and *Invite* sends the invitation when you click it.

**Is the bank always up to date?** It is as recent as the last visit of a guildmate with the addon; each tab shows its age.

**Why is a guildmate's recipe list empty?** The game does not always give recipe lists to addons, so the list usually comes from the member's own Azeroth Accord, shared when they open their profession window. If they do not use the addon, or have not opened the profession since installing it, the panel tells you what to ask them. See [[Professions and Recipes|Professions-and-Recipes]].

**Does Request craft send a message by itself?** No. It opens the chat box with the whisper already written; you read it and send it.

**Can the addon take items or gold out of the bank for me?** No. A bank request is only a note that the whole guild sees and officers answer; the item is handed over in the game, by mail, trade or at the vault.

**Is the addon available in Italian?** Yes. In an Italian game client (`itIT`) the whole interface is in Italian: the panel, the views, the Settings page and the chat lines. Every other client gets English. Names that come from the game (items, recipes, zones, guild ranks) and what players type are never translated, and the `/accord debug` report stays in English on purpose so it can be pasted in a bug report. A mistake in a translation is a normal bug: see [[Reporting Bugs and Feedback|Reporting-Bugs-and-Feedback]].

**How much space does the addon use?** In a busy guild (1000 members, 400 of them with the addon, three months of play) the saved data is about 3.5 MB and the addon uses a few tens of MB of memory while the panel has been opened. Most of it is the record of who played with whom (kept 90 days). *Settings → Saved data* shows the sizes, and **Clear guild data** frees them; the guild fills it again.

**A view says "This view could not be drawn".** Something failed while opening that one view; the other views are not affected. Click its name in the list to try again, and if it stays, send the `/accord debug` report ([[Reporting Bugs and Feedback|Reporting-Bugs-and-Feedback]]).
