# Guild addons

*Italiano: [[Addon della gilda|Guild-Addons-it]]*

> **New in `v0.9.0`**. Sharing your list is on by default ([Switching sharing off](#switching-sharing-off)). Nothing on this page has been checked in the real game.

The **Guild addons** tab shows which addons the members of your guild use, with a count for each. Open it with its tab or with `/accord addons`.

## What is shared

Each character that runs Accord publishes the list of the addons it has **loaded**:

- the **folder name** of each addon, its **version** (when it states one) and its **title** (without colours and icons);
- Blizzard's own addons are **left out**, and so are addons that are installed but switched off or not loaded;
- Accord itself is in the list, so the guild sees which versions of it are in use;
- nothing else: no addon settings, no files, nothing about your computer.

The list goes out one to two minutes after you log in, and **only if it changed** since the last time (an unchanged list is sent again after 30 days). When an addon loads while you play, the list is checked again, at most once every 10 minutes. A list holds at most **120 addons** and has a size limit: with very many addons, or long names, only the first ones in alphabetical order of the folders are sent. A list that is not renewed is removed after **60 days**.

## Who sees what

| What | Every member | Officers and the Guild Master |
|---|---|---|
| How many members use an addon, their share, the versions | yes | yes |
| **Who** uses an addon | no | yes |
| Your own entry | yes | yes |

**Hiding the names is a rule of the interface, not of the data.** The lists themselves reach the whole guild, as the other shared data does, so a member who reads the addon's saved file or uses a modified addon can see them. If you do not want your list to be seen, switch sharing off.

## The table

The line on top says out of how many members the counts are: **those who run Accord and share their list**. Members without Accord, and members who share nothing, are not known and not counted. Your characters count as **one member** (the alts are grouped as in the Roster).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Addon | the title, or the folder name; the folder is in the tooltip |
| Members | how many members have it loaded on at least one of their characters |
| Share | the same number out of the members who share |
| Versions | the most used version and `+N` for the others; the tooltip lists them with their counts |

Click a column title to sort by it (to begin with: most used first; the *Versions* column sorts by how many versions are in use). The search box at the top filters by title or folder name. The rows of your own addons are marked.

Click a row to see who uses it. An officer sees every member who does, with the characters that have it and the version; anybody else sees only their own entry.

## Switching sharing off

**Share my addon list** is **on** by default. It is on the **What I share** page and in the Contacts card of [[Settings|Settings]]. Off:

- the lists already published by your characters are **withdrawn** at once (a withdrawal is one short message), and you disappear from the counts;
- nothing more is sent;
- guildmates who are offline learn of the withdrawal when their addon syncs again; a character of yours that this computer does not hold a list for withdraws its own at its next login;
- you can still read the table, and your own addons stay marked: *loaded on this character, not in the shared counts*.

Switch it on again and your list is published at the next check.
