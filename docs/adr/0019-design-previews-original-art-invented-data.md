# 0019. Design previews: original art, invented data, drawn from the addon's texts

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-19

## Context

The wiki and the READMEs should show what the interface looks like, as Azeroth Guild Finder's do, but the addon has not run in the real client yet, so there are no screenshots to take. Azeroth Guild Finder's own previews hot-link the game's class icons from a third-party CDN when they are made and use its own parchment skin; Accord's panel is drawn with the game's own frame templates, fonts and colours ([SPEC-04 §5](../specs/SPEC-04-ui-guild-tab-shell.md)).

## Decision

1. The images are **design previews, not screenshots**, and say so in a strip at the bottom of every image. They are drawn from the addon's own layout and **texts** (`Locale/enUS.lua`, `itIT.lua`), so they cannot disagree with the interface, and regenerated by `tools/make_mockups.py` when the interface changes.
2. **Original art only**: class badges, emblem, tab and item pictures are CSS shapes and inline SVG; no game screenshot, icon, texture or font is copied or loaded from a network, and the images can be made offline. (This departs from Guild Finder, which loads class icons from a CDN at render time.)
3. **Invented data**: guild, characters, notes and contact names are made up; no real player, guild or account appears (the privacy rule of the repository applies to images too).
4. The look is the game's guild window (dark insets, bronze frames, gold headings), not Guild Finder's parchment: it is what the panel really has.
5. WebP, quality 80; the files live under `docs/specs/mockups/` (outside the addon package) and are linked with relative paths from the wiki sources and the READMEs; `tools/sync_wiki.py` copies the ones the wiki shows.
6. When the owner's in-game checks produce real screenshots, they may replace the previews of a view; the previews then stay as the drawing of the states a screenshot cannot show easily (empty, loading, officer-only).

## Update (M6.8)

Rule 4 (the look is the game's guild window) is superseded by [ADR 0021](0021-interface-follows-the-ecosystem-style-of-fdcp.md): the interface takes the ecosystem style of FDCP, so the previews are redrawn in the new look (Nebula, with a few in Aurora and Accord, plus the About page) in step M6.16. The other rules stay: design previews and not screenshots, original art, invented data, drawn from the addon's texts.

## Update (M6.16)

The previews are drawn again in the new look, with the style sheet and the components of FDCP (read only) and Accord's three themes ([`ui-style.md`](../specs/ui-style.md) §8). Rules 1, 3 and 6 stand. Changes to the others:

- Rule 2 (original art): the pictures in a preview are now the addon's own logo and the NEBULA logos of `assets/` (used with the guild's permission), and the fonts are the ones the addon ships (Outfit, JetBrains Mono, Orbitron, SIL OFL 1.1, from `AzerothAccord/Media/Fonts/`), read from the repository by the local headless browser. Still no game screenshot, icon, texture or font, and no network.
- Rule 4 (the look): superseded in M6.8; the previews show Nebula, the default theme, with a few in Aurora and in Accord. The guild window around the panel is drawn dressed in the theme as the addon dresses it (M6.18, ADR 0003 update): a themed frame, the guild's name, the cross and the side tabs as tiles, with generic line icons for the game's tabs; no game art.
- Rule 5 (files): WebP quality 80 as before; `docs/specs/mockups/html/accord.css` is generated from `tools/mockup_lib.py`, and `tools/check_docs.py` fails when a preview, the style sheet or an annotated page is missing or out of date, or an image has no scene.
- New: a comparison sheet, `docs/specs/mockups/compare/accord-vs-fdcp.webp` (`tools/make_compare.py`), puts Accord's previews beside FDCP's to check that the two addons look like one family.

## Consequences

- Positive: a manual with pictures before the first run in the game; no licensing question about game art; reproducible, light and translatable (the Italian previews come from the Italian locale).
- Negative: the previews can differ from the real window in fonts, textures and spacing, and must be kept in step with the interface by rerunning the generator (a review point of every change that moves a view).
