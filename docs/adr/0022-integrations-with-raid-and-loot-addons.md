# 0022. Integrations with raid and loot addons: one source per datum, read-only, the game calendar shared

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24 ([M10 integration rules](../roadmap/M10-calendar-raids.md#integration-rules-owner-2026-11-24), [SPEC-10 Q10](../specs/SPEC-10-risks-open-questions.md))
- Updated: 2026-11-25 (M10.1: points 11–15, the game calendar, the season roster and Raid-Helper; [SPEC-14](../specs/SPEC-14-calendar-raids-season.md)); 2026-11-25 (M10.3: point 10, the switch split into `season` and `raid`)

## Context

M10 (calendar, raids and the season roster) and M11 (loot and the guild handbook) were first planned as features of Accord alone. A review against Finland Dragon Credit Points (FDCP, the loot and raid addon of the same author) and the other loot addons that run on WoW Forever showed that they overlap: FDCP already keeps the season pool (MS1, MS2, OS), the bench rotation and the attendance per boss, and it reads the sign-ups of the game's calendar. Two addons keeping the same datum would show two truths to the guild. On 2026-11-24 the owner set the integration rules of M10 and M11 and answered SPEC-10 Q10: FDCP publishes a read-only, versioned API in its own repository. FDCP shipped it the same day (its step M8-1, its ADR 0009): the global table `FinlandDragonCreditPointsAPI`, `API_VERSION` 1.

## Decision

1. **One source per datum, chosen by the officers.** The season roster with the lineup and the bench (`raid`) and the loot data (`loot`: a member's credits and queue place, the rules summary) each belong to one addon per guild. The choice is a guild setting written only by officers and synced to every member (the record `sources` of the data set `guildcfg`, [SPEC-03 §8](../specs/SPEC-03-data-model.md#8-impostazioni-della-gilda-guildcfg)), never a guess of each client, so every member sees the same truth. Defaults: `raid` = Accord, `loot` = none.
2. **Read-only, through a documented and versioned public API only.** Accord never writes another addon's data, never reads another addon's SavedVariables or private namespace (`C_AddOns.GetAddOnLocalTable` is not used), and never loads, enables or disables another addon.
3. **Three levels.** Level 0, detection: the addon is loaded, its version, and a way to open it. Level 1, reading: only through the addon's public API, and only when its version is the one Accord knows; any other version falls back to level 0 without an error. Level 2, a two-way exchange: never. Today only FDCP has level 1 (`API_VERSION == 1`); Gargul, RaidCore, WLC, Forever Loot Manager, NostalgiaForeverLC and RollForMe stay at level 0, and their folder names are confirmed in game before they are trusted.
4. **Only loot and raid-roster addons.** Other guild-management addons are not detected and not read.
5. **No loot engine and no attendance in Accord.** FDCP does not expose attendance either; nothing Accord shows from FDCP counts presence at raids.
6. **The game calendar is the shared ground** for events and sign-ups (M10.5): every addon then sees the same answers. FDCP reads the sign-ups of the game calendar and charges a credit to a raider who did not sign up, so an answer kept only in Accord would cost that raider; Accord writes the player's own answer into the calendar and keeps in its own data only what the calendar cannot hold (the role chosen for an event). Events that live only in Accord exist only on a client without a usable calendar, and are marked "Not in the game calendar" ([SPEC-14 §5](../specs/SPEC-14-calendar-raids-season.md#5-eventi-e-iscrizioni-sul-calendario-del-gioco-m105)).
7. **Load order is not a dependency.** The TOC names FDCP in `## OptionalDeps` (a hint that loads it first when it is installed), and Accord looks for the table again at `PLAYER_LOGIN` and on `ADDON_LOADED` of a known folder. Each getter is guarded; `nil` means "not on this client", never "empty".
8. **Callbacks only while needed.** Accord registers FDCP's callbacks only while the guild chose FDCP as a source and level 1 holds, and unregisters them when the source changes away from FDCP (M11.7).
9. **Where it shows** (M11.7): the officers choose the sources in the **Raid** view, which also shows FDCP's season pool, the night's lineup and bench and the rules summary; the member card shows a member's credits and queue place. Until the handbook's loot page exists (M11.6), the rules summary lives in the Raid view only. Nothing read from FDCP is saved or sent.

10. **Switched off until M10 and M11 are complete** (2026-11-25, no half-finished features in a release): the switches of `Core/Features.lua` are off in the shipped code. Since M10.3 there are two, so that M10 can ship as `v0.10.0` before M11: `season` (M10: detection, the choice of the season roster's source, `guildcfg`, the season roster and the later M10 steps; points 11–15) and `raid` (M11: the Raid view, `/accord raid`, the loot source, the member card row); `raid` on turns `season` on too. With `season` off nothing of this decision happens: no detection, no choice of a source, `guildcfg` neither accepted nor sent, no FDCP call or callback, no Season or Raid view, command or member card row. M10.10 turned `season` on (2026-11-26); `raid` stays off until M11 is complete.
11. **Which data follow the source** (M10.1, [SPEC-14 §3](../specs/SPEC-14-calendar-raids-season.md#3-la-fonte-e-i-fornitori-m102-m103)). The source `raid` decides the season roster (declarations of MS1, MS2, OS and a note) and the lineup with the bench. With `accord` they are Accord's data sets `season` and `raidev`; with `fdcp` they are FDCP's, read-only, and Accord accepts no declaration or composition of its own. Events and sign-ups, the Raid-Helper import, the availability map and rallies do not depend on the source. Changing the source hides Accord's own data without deleting it. Both providers answer the same interface, and what FDCP gives only to officers is said in a line, never shown as an empty column.
12. **The season roster: own entry only, locked for the season.** Only the account of a character writes its declaration, officers included (a forged entry is refused); MS1, MS2 and OS do not change for the season after a short correction window; a new season starts empty and the old ones stay readable. The declaration is not a sign-up and not attendance.
13. **Accord writes into the game calendar only what a person decided, on a click.** A member's own answer, from their click; an officer's import or lineup, after an explicit confirmation, only for the matched members and only where the client allows it; never over an answer the member already gave in the game. Accord never writes another addon's data: the calendar belongs to the game, not to FDCP.
14. **Discord only by pasting.** Raid-Helper events come in as the export (JSON or CSV) an officer copies and pastes; the parser runs nothing from the text and refuses what it does not understand. Only members matched by Discord name are synced; the unmatched names and the hand matches stay on the importing officer's computer and the former are dropped after the event. Accord never contacts Discord or a bot ([ADR 0001](0001-client-only-addon-guild-only-data.md)).
15. **No attendance, shared traffic.** The availability map counts online presence only, never presence at raids; nothing of M10 records who was at a raid. Accord's traffic counts against the same server limits as FDCP's: Accord stays quiet during boss fights, and M10 must fit in the average budget of SPEC-02 §4 ([SPEC-09 §7](../specs/SPEC-09-quality-release.md#7-budget-di-traffico-condiviso-m10)). What Accord would still like from FDCP's API is a request document for the FDCP repository ([FDCP API requests](../integrations/FDCP-API-requests.md)); the FDCP repository is never changed from here.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep events and sign-ups in Accord only.** FDCP and the game's own interface would not see them, and FDCP would charge a credit to raiders who answered only in Accord.
- **Fetch Raid-Helper events from its web API.** An addon has no internet access, and a companion app or a bot breaks the client-only rule (ADR 0001).
- **Let officers fix a member's season declaration.** Two writers of one entry, and loot follows the declared spec: only the member decides what they declare (SPEC-10 Q20).
- **Each client guesses the source** (FDCP if it is loaded, else Accord). Members with and without FDCP would see different rosters.
- **Read FDCP's SavedVariables.** No contract: their layout changes without notice, and they hold attendance that FDCP keeps for officers only.
- **A two-way exchange** (Accord writing declarations into FDCP). Two writers of one datum, and FDCP would depend on Accord.
- **A hard dependency** (`## Dependencies`). Accord would not load without FDCP; the two addons stay independent ([ADR 0021](0021-interface-follows-the-ecosystem-style-of-fdcp.md)).

## Consequences

- Positive: one truth per datum for the whole guild; FDCP can change its internals freely while `API_VERSION` holds; a new reader version is a small, tested change.
- Negative: a member's client sees less than an officer's (FDCP's snapshot has no MS2, OS, availability, night or guild rules), and Accord must say so instead of showing empty lists; the other loot addons offer no API, so Accord only points to them; nothing is verified in the real client until the owner's checks (the two addons loaded together, a `/reload`, the load order).
- M10.1: the calendar calls (answers, creating a guild event, setting another member's state) are not verified in the real client and the dump lacks the values of `Enum.CalendarStatus` ([SPEC-10 R15](../specs/SPEC-10-risks-open-questions.md)); Raid-Helper's export can change without notice (R16).
